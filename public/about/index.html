What is Metal Machine Trio?
Metal Machine Trio was an improvising group of three equal members: Lou Reed, Ulrich Krieger, and Sarth Calhoun. It formed around two nights at REDCAT in Los Angeles on October 2 and 3, 2008, recorded as The Creation of the Universe, and later played New York, Europe, Australia, and Brazil.
What did Sarth Calhoun do on Lulu?
Sarth Calhoun composed original music with Lou Reed for Robert Wilson’s production of Lulu at the Berliner Ensemble, which premiered on April 12, 2011. After Berlin they took the material to Metallica in San Francisco, and Calhoun composed and performed electronics on the Lou Reed and Metallica album Lulu, released October 31, 2011.
What is “Junior Dad”?
“Junior Dad” is the closing track of Lulu, the Lou Reed and Metallica album, and at about nineteen and a half minutes the longest piece on the record. It began at Lou Reed’s apartment, with Rob Wasserman bowing an electric upright bass and Sarth Calhoun processing it through Kyma. That first session became the basis of the track.
