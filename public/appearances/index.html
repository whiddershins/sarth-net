Live

Appearances

A record of shows and installations. The Creation of the Universe comes from two nights at REDCAT, October 2–3, 2008. The later ambisonic installation uses a different recording, made at the Gramercy show on April 24, 2009.

Spine

  1. Metal Machine Trio (Unclassified) at REDCAT, Los Angeles · Oct 2–3, 2008. World premiere billed as Unclassified: Lou Reed and Ulrich Krieger. Three performances: Thu 2 Oct 8:30pm, added late show Thu 2 Oct 10:30pm, Fri 3 Oct 8:30pm. Sarth on Continuum / live processing. Official live album The Creation of the Universe is the unedited recording of those two nights. confirmed
  2. Metal Machine Trio, Blender Theater at Gramercy, New York · Apr 23–24, 2009. First NYC stand. John Zorn sat in on the 24th. This night’s recording later became the CSULB / Cranbrook ambisonic installation. confirmed
  3. Yellow Pony with Lou Reed and Laurie Anderson · Europe, Jul and Aug–Sep 2009. Eight-city tour billed The Yellow Pony and Other Songs and Stories. Sarth on Continuum and live voice re-sampling, center stage. Two short tours. confirmed
  4. Lou Reed at Lollapalooza, Grant Park, Chicago · Aug 9, 2009. Day three. Metal Machine Trio played a ten minute noise interlude inside Reed’s set. Sarth on Continuum and live processing. confirmed
  5. Metal Machine Trio Europe · “A Night of Deep Noise,” Apr 17–30, 2010. Nine announced dates, UK through Palma de Mallorca. Mallorca is MM3, not Yellow Pony. confirmed
  6. Metal Machine Trio, Sydney Opera House (Opera Theatre) · May 30, 2010. Vivid LIVE. Sarth on the bill. Anderson’s Transitory Life was a separate Drama Theatre program, 1–2 Jun. confirmed
  7. Metal Machine Trio, Sesc Pinheiros, São Paulo · Nov 20–21, 2010. Two nights. confirmed
  8. Ambisonic installation The Creation of the Universe · CSULB University Art Museum, Long Beach, 27 Jan–Apr 15, 2012; restaged Cranbrook Art Museum, Bloomfield Hills, Nov 21, 2015–Mar 26, 2016. Playback of the Gramercy 2009 recording, not a live Sarth performance. confirmed

The public record shows no Metal Machine Trio concerts after São Paulo. Wikipedia’s 2008–2013 span follows the life of the project through Reed’s death; it does not point to additional shows.

Lulu

Studio: electronics on Lulu (2011). Continuum drones on Reed’s pre-Metallica tapes (Fricke). No public live date with Calhoun was found. Reed+Metallica played television without a Calhoun credit.
