# Citations

Every outbound link on this site, grouped by the page it appears on. Machine readable: [citations.json](/citations.json).

## What first-party means here

This site is a primary source. Sarth Calhoun is the author, the publisher and the subject, so there is no gap between the source and the person describing it. Wikipedia and Wikidata require independent reliable sources because they are written by strangers who cannot verify him. That standard belongs to those projects.

Links are here so a reader or an agent can follow a claim to further evidence, and so entities resolve unambiguously. Where a claim carries no link, no public source has been found for it. That is not a measure of how reliable it is.

Resolve the person through [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarth_Calhoun), [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q7424654), [MusicBrainz](https://musicbrainz.org/artist/e6ac65c7-146f-44bc-9acd-002633185c4f), [Discogs](https://www.discogs.com/artist/2310400-Sarth-Calhoun) or [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/6uUtOazhiVXfNU8KAglFv9). The full set is in the JSON-LD `sameAs` on every page.
