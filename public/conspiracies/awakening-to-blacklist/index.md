# Awakening (To Blacklist)

A track from The Book of Sarth that starts as the hum of a badly plugged-in cable and turns into a melody.

## What it is

A track from [The Book of Sarth](/conspiracies/book-of-sarth/), my graphic novel and concept album built as one iPad app, and the first track on [*Transmissions from the Book of Sarth*](/transmissions/transmissions-from-the-book-of-sarth/). One of my favorite things about this track is the morph from the hum to a rhythmic/pitched pattern.

There’s a scene in ‘Book of Sarth’ where the office workers are coming out of a daze, they are transitioning back in to reality, and the hum transforming really ended up matching this perfectly. (I don’t know which idea came first.)
