# Hidden Books, Hidden Stories

Lou Reed’s pavilion at the New York Photo Festival, May 2010, and its slideshow.

*Hidden Books, Hidden Stories* was [Lou Reed](/conspirators/lou-reed/)’s pavilion at the [New York Photo Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Photo_Festival) in Dumbo, Brooklyn, May 12 to 16, 2010. Its slideshow ran at St. Ann’s Warehouse, mixed and authored by Daniel Power in FotoMagico, and the festival put it on Vimeo on May 19, 2010: fifteen and a half minutes. The upload’s tags name me alongside Lou, Daniel Power and St. Ann’s.

It was two weeks before [Vivid LIVE](/conspiracies/vivid-live/) in Sydney, the festival Lou and Laurie Anderson curated that same spring.

## The slideshow

Vogue Italia described the pavilion on May 17, 2010 as an installation of book boxes and beloved books. The slideshow is the part that survives online: the New York Photo Festival’s own upload, which the festival brought back in a 2012 post as a flashback to its 2010 edition, and which Boinx, the makers of FotoMagico, wrote up the week it went up.

Dates, venue and credits are the festival’s own description on Vimeo, repeated by Boinx on May 20, 2010 and by the festival in February 2012.
