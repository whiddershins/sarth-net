Conspiracies

Bands, tours, records, theater pieces, films, and machines.

  1. Lou Reed’s song bandLou wanted fresh sounds. Sarth became the place he went for them.
  2. Lou Reed spring 2008 U.S. tourNorthampton, a bus south, Beale Street, and the beginning of the band.
  3. Metal Machine TrioThree people walked into REDCAT without a band name and came out with one.
  4. LuluInterstitial music at Lou’s house became a theater score, then something else again with Metallica.
  5. Lou Reed’s 2011 European tourEight people onstage across the festival summer. Two posts from the road are most of what survives.
  6. The Creation of the Universe, ambisonic installationA trio recording turned into a room you walk into. He has never heard it.
  7. Reflections, Vol. 1Improvised duets, named from a line in a review of a Metal Machine Trio show.
  8. “I Just Believe in Christmas”An argument with the neighbors about squirrels in the attic became a song.
  9. “Awakening (To Blacklist)”Sixty-cycle hum off a badly plugged-in cable, turned into a melody.
  10. “Junior Dad”The nineteen-minute closing track of Lou Reed and Metallica’s Lulu, 2011. Built from a recording made at Reed’s apartment before the album existed.
