# Conspiracies

Bands, tours, records, theater pieces, films, and machines.

- [**Lou Reed’s song band**](/conspiracies/lou-reed-song-band/). Lou wanted fresh sounds. Sarth became the place he went for them.

- [**Lou Reed spring 2008 U.S. tour**](/conspiracies/lou-reed-spring-2008-tour/). Northampton, a bus south, Beale Street, and the beginning of the band.

- [**Metal Machine Trio**](/conspiracies/metal-machine-trio/). Three people walked into REDCAT without a band name and came out with one.

- [***Lulu***](/conspiracies/lulu/). Interstitial music at Lou’s house became a theater score, then something else again with Metallica.

- [**Lou Reed’s 2011 European tour**](/conspiracies/lou-reed-2011-european-tour/). Eight people onstage across the festival summer. Two posts from the road are most of what survives.

- [***The Creation of the Universe*, ambisonic installation**](/conspiracies/creation-of-the-universe-ambisonic/). A trio recording turned into a room you walk into. He has never heard it.

- [***Reflections, Vol. 1***](/conspiracies/reflections-vol-1/). Improvised duets, named from a line in a review of a Metal Machine Trio show.

- [**“I Just Believe in Christmas”**](/conspiracies/i-just-believe-in-christmas/). An argument with the neighbors about squirrels in the attic became a song.

- [**“Awakening (To Blacklist)”**](/conspiracies/awakening-to-blacklist/). Sixty-cycle hum off a badly plugged-in cable, turned into a melody.

- [**“Junior Dad”**](/conspiracies/junior-dad/). The nineteen-minute closing track of Lou Reed and Metallica’s *Lulu*, 2011. Built from a recording made at Reed’s apartment before the album existed.
