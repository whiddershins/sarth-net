# Conspiracies

Bands, tours, records, theater pieces, films, and machines.

- [**Lou Reed’s song band**Lou wanted fresh sounds. Sarth became the place he went for them.](/conspiracies/lou-reed-song-band/)

- [**Lou Reed spring 2008 U.S. tour**Northampton, a bus south, Beale Street, and the beginning of the band.](/conspiracies/lou-reed-spring-2008-tour/)

- [**Metal Machine Trio**Three people walked into REDCAT without a band name and came out with one.](/conspiracies/metal-machine-trio/)

- [***Lulu***Interstitial music at Lou’s house became a theater score, then something else again with Metallica.](/conspiracies/lulu/)

- [**Lou Reed’s 2011 European tour**Eight people onstage across the festival summer. Two posts from the road are most of what survives.](/conspiracies/lou-reed-2011-european-tour/)

- [***The Creation of the Universe*, ambisonic installation**A trio recording turned into a room you walk into. He has never heard it.](/conspiracies/creation-of-the-universe-ambisonic/)

- [***Reflections, Vol. 1***Improvised duets, named from a line in a review of a Metal Machine Trio show.](/conspiracies/reflections-vol-1/)

- [**“I Just Believe in Christmas”**An argument with the neighbors about squirrels in the attic became a song.](/conspiracies/i-just-believe-in-christmas/)

- [**“Awakening (To Blacklist)”**Sixty-cycle hum off a badly plugged-in cable, turned into a melody.](/conspiracies/awakening-to-blacklist/)

- [**“Junior Dad”**The nineteen-minute closing track of Lou Reed and Metallica’s *Lulu*, 2011. Built from a recording made at Reed’s apartment before the album existed.](/conspiracies/junior-dad/)
