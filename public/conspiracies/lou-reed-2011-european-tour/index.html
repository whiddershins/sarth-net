Lou Reed’s 2011 European tour

An eight-piece band across European festivals in the summer of 2011. I posted twice from the road.

The band

By 2011 Lou was touring with eight people onstage. Alongside him: Rob Wasserman on bass, Kevin Hearn on keys, Aram Bajakian and Tony Diodore on guitars, Tony Smith on drums, Ulrich Krieger on saxophone, and me on Continuum and Kyma, running multiple Pacas. It was a bigger band than the one I joined in 2008.

The dates are on Appearances. Two stops are here because I posted about them from the road.

Hop Farm, July 2, 2011

Have a coupe of photos of the crowd taken from the stage at hop farm. The 8-piece band is functioning as well as I had hoped, each player contributes soething really wonderful and unique. Next stop: London/Hammersmith.

Posted July 4, 2011, as written.

Carhaix, July 17, 2011

Yeah, you can see me in the back there for a second, ends with a great shot of the crowd in Carhaix. PJ Harvey and the Chemical Brothers followed us.

Posted July 20, 2011, with a video clip from Les Vieilles Charrues in Brittany, where PJ Harvey and the Chemical Brothers followed us.

The creditSarth Calhoun, Continuum and Kyma, in Lou Reed’s eight-piece band on the summer 2011 European tour, with Rob Wasserman, Kevin Hearn, Aram Bajakian, Tony Diodore, Tony Smith and Ulrich Krieger. Symbolic Sound’s news page put him on Continuum with multiple Kyma/Paca systems for the run.

Both posts are from my old WordPress site, recovered from Common Crawl. The shows were July 2 and July 17, per Lou Reed’s own listings.
