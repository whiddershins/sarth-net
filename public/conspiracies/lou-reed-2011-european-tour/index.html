Lou Reed’s 2011 European tour

An eight-piece band across European festivals in the summer of 2011. I posted twice from the road.

The band

By 2011 Lou was touring with eight people onstage. Alongside him: Rob Wasserman on bass, Kevin Hearn on keys, Aram Bajakian and Tony Diodore on guitars, Tony Smith on drums, Ulrich Krieger on saxophone, and me on Continuum and Kyma, running multiple Pacas. It was a bigger band than the one I joined in 2008.

The dates are on Sightings. Two stops are here because I posted about them from the road.

The rigSarth Calhoun, performing on Continuum as part of Lou Reed’s band, is touring Europe this July, controlling multiple Kyma/Paca systems for sound synthesis and processing submixes from the band. Symbolic Sound, July 26, 2011

The creditSarth Calhoun, Continuum and Kyma, in Lou Reed’s eight-piece band on the summer 2011 European tour, with Rob Wasserman, Kevin Hearn, Aram Bajakian, Tony Diodore, Tony Smith and Ulrich Krieger.
