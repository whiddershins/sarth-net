# Lou Reed’s 2011 European tour

An eight-piece band across European festivals in the summer of 2011. I posted twice from the road.

## The band

By 2011 Lou was touring with eight people onstage. Alongside him: [Rob Wasserman](/conspirators/rob-wasserman/) on bass, [Kevin Hearn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Hearn) on keys, [Aram Bajakian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aram_Bajakian) and [Tony Diodore](/conspirators/tony-diodore/) on guitars, Tony Smith on drums, [Ulrich Krieger](/conspirators/ulrich-krieger/) on saxophone, and me on [Continuum and Kyma](/devices/), running multiple Pacas. It was a bigger band than the [one I joined in 2008](/conspiracies/lou-reed-song-band/).

The dates are on [Appearances](/appearances/). Two stops are here because I posted about them from the road.

## Hop Farm, July 2, 2011

> Have a coupe of photos of the crowd taken from the stage at hop farm. The 8-piece band is functioning as well as I had hoped, each player contributes soething really wonderful and unique. Next stop: London/Hammersmith.

Posted July 4, 2011, as written.

## Carhaix, July 17, 2011

> Yeah, you can see me in the back there for a second, ends with a great shot of the crowd in Carhaix. PJ Harvey and the Chemical Brothers followed us.

Posted July 20, 2011, with a video clip from Les Vieilles Charrues in Brittany, where [PJ Harvey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PJ_Harvey) and the [Chemical Brothers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Chemical_Brothers) followed us.
