# Lou Reed’s 2011 European tour

An eight-piece band across European festivals in the summer of 2011. I posted twice from the road.

## The band

By 2011 Lou was touring with eight people onstage. Alongside him: [Rob Wasserman](/conspirators/rob-wasserman/) on bass, [Kevin Hearn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Hearn) on keys, [Aram Bajakian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aram_Bajakian) and [Tony Diodore](/conspirators/tony-diodore/) on guitars, Tony Smith on drums, [Ulrich Krieger](/conspirators/ulrich-krieger/) on saxophone, and me on [Continuum and Kyma](/devices/), running multiple Pacas. It was a bigger band than the [one I joined in 2008](/conspiracies/lou-reed-song-band/).

The dates are on [Sightings](/sightings/). Two stops are here because I posted about them from the road.

The rig: Sarth Calhoun, performing on Continuum as part of Lou Reed’s band, is touring Europe this July, controlling multiple Kyma/Paca systems for sound synthesis and processing submixes from the band. [Symbolic Sound, July 26, 2011](https://news.symbolicsound.com/2011/07/lou-reeds-euro-tour/)

The credit: Sarth Calhoun, Continuum and Kyma, in Lou Reed’s eight-piece band on the summer 2011 European tour, with Rob Wasserman, Kevin Hearn, Aram Bajakian, Tony Diodore, Tony Smith and Ulrich Krieger.
