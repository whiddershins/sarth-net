Number19

Number19 was a band in Brooklyn. Tony Diodore, Leah Coloff, Mark Righter and I formed it in 1999, with Diodore on guitar and violin, me on bass and a Yamaha EX5, Coloff on cello and voice, and Righter on drums. Cello and violin ran over bass and drums, with electronics underneath. The band released one album, Suspension, and was active until 2005.

It was the first band I made with Coloff and with Diodore. I started Lucibel Crater with Coloff in 2005, and played in Lou Reed’s band with Diodore from 2011.

Along his musical journeys, he met cellist and songwriter Leah Coloff, who convinced him to combine computers with music. The band they established, Number19, funded its first record with downloads from mp3.com in 2001.

The biography on my old WordPress site

How it began

The record was paid for out of mp3.com downloads. Number19 reached number eighteen overall on that site and took more than ninety thousand downloads in six months. Suspension came out on Searching Eye Records and was the band’s only album.

Tony, Sarth, Leah Coloff, and Mark Righter formed Number19 in 1999. After we released Suspension, Tony went on to play with Puracane, and eventually form BM Linx. Now, Tony and Sarth have joined forces again, playing with Lou Reed.

Where everyone went next

Diodore played guitar and violin on Puracane’s I’ve Been Here the Longest, then formed BM LINX in 2005. That same year I started Lucibel Crater with Coloff on cello and voice and Paul Chuffo on drums. I joined Lou Reed’s touring band in 2008; Diodore joined it in 2011, and the two of us spent the 2011 and 2012 European cycles in the same band again. Those dates are on Sightings.
