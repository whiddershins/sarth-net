# Number19

[Number19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number19) was a band in Brooklyn. [Tony Diodore](/conspirators/tony-diodore/), [Leah Coloff](/conspirators/leah-coloff/), Mark Righter and I formed it in 1999, with Diodore on guitar and violin, me on bass and a [Yamaha EX5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamaha_EX5), Coloff on cello and voice, and Righter on drums. Cello and violin ran over bass and drums, with electronics underneath. The band released one album, *Suspension*, and was active until 2005.

It was the first band I made with Coloff and with Diodore. I started [Lucibel Crater](/conspiracies/lucibel-crater/) with Coloff in 2005, and played in [Lou Reed’s band](/conspiracies/lou-reed-song-band/) with Diodore from 2011.

## How it began

From the biography on my old site: Along his musical journeys, he met cellist and songwriter Leah Coloff, who convinced him to combine computers with music. The band they established, Number19, funded its first record with downloads from mp3.com in 2001.

## Suspension

[Embedded player](https://embed.music.apple.com/us/album/suspension/274828088)

The record: [*Suspension*](/transmissions/suspension/), 2001. Nine tracks. On [Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/album/suspension/274828088) and [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/album/0EBi9RJO4j2cGeL0CPtYW5), credited to Number 19.

The credit: Sarth Calhoun, bass and Yamaha EX5, in Number19 with Tony Diodore, Leah Coloff and Mark Righter, 1999 to 2005; on *Suspension*, Searching Eye Records, 2001.

The record was paid for out of [mp3.com](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mp3.com) downloads. Number19 reached number eighteen overall on that site and took more than ninety thousand downloads in six months. *Suspension* came out on Searching Eye Records and was the band’s only album.

## Where everyone went next

Diodore played guitar and violin on [Puracane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puracane)’s *I’ve Been Here the Longest*, then formed [BM LINX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BM_Linx) in 2005. That same year I started Lucibel Crater with Coloff on cello and voice and [Paul Chuffo](/conspirators/paul-chuffo/) on drums. I joined [Lou Reed](/conspirators/lou-reed/)’s touring band in 2008; Diodore joined it in 2011, and the two of us spent the 2011 and 2012 European cycles in the same band again. Those dates are on [Appearances](/appearances/).
