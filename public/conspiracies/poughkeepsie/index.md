# Poughkeepsie

Joe Kelly’s short film, 2018. Its main theme is the melody of my “Softly Questioning.”

*Poughkeepsie* is a 32-minute short film written and directed by [Joe Kelly](/conspirators/joe-kelly/), the comics writer of *I Kill Giants* and co-creator of *Ben 10*. It is a drama about George Dawes, a man with late-stage Alzheimer’s who finds he can go back into his memories. Funded on [Kickstarter](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/1749833744/poughkeepsie-a-short-film) between November 12 and December 11, 2015, by 277 backers, it was shot in Nassau County and premiered at the Long Island Film Festival on July 15, 2018.

## The melody

The film’s main melody is the melody of “Softly Questioning,” the third track of [*Reflections, Vol. 1*](/conspiracies/reflections-vol-1/): an improvised duet for Continuum Fingerboard and [Lou Petto](/conspirators/lou-petto/)’s drums, recorded in one take with no editing and released in November 2015, the month the film’s Kickstarter closed. I wrote about the connection when I revisited the record in November 2020, and on May 20, 2025 I released “Softly Questioning (Reprise),” a return to the piece.
