# Reflections, Vol. 1

My first release of improvised duets, named after a line in a review of a Metal Machine Trio show.

## The record

*Reflections, Vol. 1 (A Noise Akin to a Flight of Deranged Swallows in Combat)* is my first release of improvised duets, and the 4th piece is a trio, which breaks the rule. It came out in November 2015.

[Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/album/reflections-vol-1-a-noise-akin-to-a-flight/1061852160) · [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-Sq3OSve1Ls)

## The absurdly long name

The name came from my friend Scott Hampton, also known as Exaltron, who took it from a review of one of my [Metal Machine Trio](/conspiracies/metal-machine-trio/) performances. I love it, and I loved the idea of having a band with an absurdly long name.

The reviewer was describing what I was doing with a Continuum. Exaltron is featured on the last track, playing trumpet, guitar, guitar-bass, and live looping madness.

## Softly Questioning

The third track is called Softly Questioning. Its main melody is the one used in the film *Poughkeepsie*, written and directed by Joe Kelly.

From Sarth’s note on the record, November 20, 2015. It is also where the Joe Kelly film is named as *Poughkeepsie*.
