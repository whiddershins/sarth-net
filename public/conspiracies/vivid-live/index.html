Vivid LIVE 2010

Vivid LIVE 2010 was a two week festival at the Sydney Opera House, running from May 27 to June 11, curated by Lou Reed and Laurie Anderson. Its stated focus was improvisation and collaboration. The program ran from large solo concerts to small rooms, talks and free events, and opened with Anderson’s lighting of the Opera House sails. ABC News reported the curation in March that year.

I played across the two weeks: Metal Machine Trio at the Opera Theatre, the noise band at the opening, and about a dozen unplanned jams.

Noise Night

Noise Night was May 31, 2010, in the Opera Theatre, with Zond, Melt-Banana, Oren Ambarchi, Boris with Oren Ambarchi, Rice Corpse, Marc Ribot with Bardo Pond, Night Terrors, and Yasuko and Ichirou from Melt-Banana, and I played in the closing sets and the encore with Lou Reed, Laurie Anderson and Marc Ribot.

A concert recording has the notes. My photo post from the night, “Photos of Noise Night, Sydney” (July 10, 2010), survives as its captions and tags, but the two photos themselves were not recovered.

The curation and the dates are from contemporary coverage. The noise band and the jams are my own recollection, September 22, 2026, and the Noise Night lineup is from the recording’s notes and my 2010 post.
