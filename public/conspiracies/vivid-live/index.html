Vivid LIVE 2010

Vivid LIVE 2010 was a two week festival at the Sydney Opera House, running from 27 May to 11 June, curated by Lou Reed and Laurie Anderson. Its stated focus was improvisation and collaboration. The program ran from large solo concerts to small rooms, talks and free events, and opened with Anderson’s lighting of the Opera House sails. ABC News reported the curation in March that year.

It is on this site as a conspiracy rather than only an appearance because it was not a festival booking. It was a project built by two of Sarth’s closest collaborators, and he played across it rather than once inside it.

A fifty-person noise band

We had basically a fifty person noise band for the opening.

Then about a dozen jams

I played something like twelve unplanned jams at that festival.

Metal Machine Trio played the Opera Theatre on 30 May, which is the one date that made it into the public record. It is listed on Appearances. The rest of it was not announced and is not written down anywhere.

The curation and the dates are from contemporary coverage. The fifty-person opening band and the unplanned jams are Sarth’s own account, given 22 September 2026.
