Yellow Pony

Yellow Pony was a performance project built around Lou Reed and Laurie Anderson, billed as The Yellow Pony and Other Songs and Stories. It ran as two short European tours in 2009, one in July and one across August and September, eight cities in all: Sant Feliu de Guíxols, Santiago de Compostela, Madrid, Copenhagen, Manchester, Helsinki, Frankfurt and Paris.

The two titans faced each other – Anderson (standing) on violin and electronics, and Reed (seated) on various guitars – with electronic sound manipulator Sarth Calhoun between them.

Dave Simpson on the Manchester night, The Guardian, July 20, 2009

What I did there

In 2009 I had the unique opportunity to do two short tours with Lou Reed and Laurie Anderson. I played continuum fingerboard and did live resampling of their voices. Also, I got to stand in the center of the stage, like a drummer. It was a wonderful experience, and it is also when I fell in love with Spain.

My role on that tour was to act as a bridge between the wildness of Lou and the grace of Laurie.

It was a lovely two part tour that included outdoor performances in northern Spain, as well as my first visit to Copenhagen where I bought a really awesome plaid jacket that I wore until it was in tatters.

The press conference

The first tour opened at the Festival de la Porta Ferrada in Sant Feliu de Guíxols on July 10, 2009. That morning the three of us sat a press conference at the Curhotel Hipócrates. El Punt Avui noted me beside them, saying nothing.

My account is from the Yellow Pony page of my old WordPress site, recovered from the Wayback Machine, and from what I added on September 23, 2026. The dates are Lou Reed’s own announcement.
