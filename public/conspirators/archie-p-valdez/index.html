Archie P. Valdez

Illustrator of The Book of Sarth, with Tom Hall.

Archie P. Valdez is an illustrator and concept artist credited, with Tom Hall, for the illustrations of The Book of Sarth (The Gralbum Collective, 2012). Two of his pieces for it, Boxy Barren and Heroes on Horses, have been on this site since 2015.

Book of Sarth

His illustrations are part of Book of Sarth, the graphic novel and concept album I released as an iPad app in October 2012, drawn with Alex Wyly and Tom Hall.
