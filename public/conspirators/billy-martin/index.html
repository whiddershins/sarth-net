Billy Martin

Drummer of Medeski Martin & Wood, and a visual artist. His Omnisphere was a gralbum in the making.

Billy Martin (born 1963, New York) is a drummer, percussionist, composer and visual artist, one third of Medeski Martin & Wood since 1991. He runs Amulet Records, played with John Lurie’s Lounge Lizards, and has published Riddim: Clave of African Origin and Wandering; his prints, paintings and drawings have been shown at the Cartagena Biennial and The Drawing Center. In 2026 he completed a Watermill Center residency with The Start of Wind, a theater work for a bamboo orchestra he built.

The illustrations behind Billy Martin’s Omnisphere echoed of a Xenakis score. Bits of musical staff decorated with half-moons, C-clefs, and spiders floated in space.

Ariel Bitran, Stereophile, April 23, 2014

Omnisphere

The Gralbum Collective listed Omnisphere, his drawings with his music, as a coming title, and a preview was on the iPads at the launch on April 9, 2014. My old WordPress site had a conspirator page for him.
