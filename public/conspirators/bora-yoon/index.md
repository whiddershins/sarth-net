# Bora Yoon

Composer, vocalist and sound artist. Her *Sunken Cathedral* was one of the first five gralbums, and she played the launch with me.

[Bora Yoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bora_Yoon_(American_musician)) (born 1980, Chicago) is a Korean-American composer, vocalist and sound artist, a TED2014 Fellow, who builds immersive soundscapes from voice, electronics, and found objects and instruments from many cultures and centuries. Her multimedia album *Sunken Cathedral* came out on Innova in 2014, with a release performance at Asia Society, New York, on April 23, 2014, featuring the kinetic sculptures of U-Ram Choe. She holds a PhD in composition from Princeton and teaches at Reed College.

## The Gralbum Collective

*Sunken Cathedral* was one of the five titles in the store when [The Gralbum Collective](/conspiracies/gralbum-collective/) launched on April 9, 2014: her “O Pastor Animarum,” with a mechanical sculpture by U-Ram Choe and video by [Jacob McCoy](/conspirators/jacob-mccoy/) and Adam Larsen. She and [Adam Matta](/conspirators/adam-matta/) played the downstairs stage at the launch with me. Two weeks later my bio was in the program for her *Sunken Cathedral* performance at Asia Society, whose projection design was by Adam Larsen and Jacob McCoy of the collective.
