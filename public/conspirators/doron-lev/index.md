# Doron Lev

Drummer, MC and illustrator. Drums on “I Just Believe in Christmas,” a guest on Beautiful Tornado, and three films at Third Wall Studio in 2026.

Doron Lev is a drummer, MC and illustrator, born in Miami to Israeli parents and based in Brooklyn, with a BFA in illustration from the School of Visual Arts. He leads the band Uglybraine, was music director for Rennie Harris’s *Funkedified* at the New Victory Theater in 2018 and for the Michael Jackson tribute *Invincible*, has toured with American Music Abroad, and has stage credits from *Break! The Urban Funk Spectacular* to Radio City’s *Spring Spectacular*. His visual work includes commissioned portraits, logos and community murals. He is an artist in residence at [Third Wall Studio](https://www.thirdwallstudio.com/).

## Drums, a podcast, and three films

He is one of the three people I asked to help finish the words of “[I Just Believe in Christmas](/conspiracies/i-just-believe-in-christmas/),” and he played the drums on it, at Figure 8 in August 2015. The first episode of [Beautiful Tornado](/transmissions/beautiful-tornado/), the podcast I made with [Dom Bouffard](/conspirators/dominic-bouffard/), is called “Waiting for Doron”; he is the guest on the second. “[Sometimes You Need Sun](/transmissions/singles/),” released in 2022 as Catfish Calhoun and Friends, is with him.

At [Third Wall Studio](/conspiracies/third-wall-studio/) three of his films came out in 2026, all made in [Burlap](/conspiracies/burlap/): *Truck Stop Love* and *Pigeon Problems* in February and *Watching Shadows* in April.
