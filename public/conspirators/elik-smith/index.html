Elik Smith

The artist of The Book of Sarth. Credited as Alex Smith.

the art is beautiful unto itself, each page filled with inset images that shift, fade, or change.

Bryan Bishop, The Verge, January 3, 2013

Elik Smith, credited on The Gralbum Collective’s title page as Alex Smith, is the artist of The Book of Sarth (2012): “Art and Story: Alex Smith,” alongside illustrations by Tom Hall and Archie P. Valdez. Marc Urselli’s review in Chain D.L.K. described the artwork as passing through several styles, from soft cartoon images to stylised vector work, treated black-and-white photographs and, by the epilogue, pixellated, almost art deco paintings.

Book of Sarth

He drew Book of Sarth, my first full-length solo release, two and a half years in the making. He is the second of the four people I thanked when it was finished: Elik Smith is a genius.

The creditElik Smith (credited as Alex Smith), art and story, and Sarth Calhoun, music, on The Book of Sarth, The Gralbum Collective, October 2012.
