# Elik Smith

The artist of *The Book of Sarth*. Credited as Alex Smith.

> the art is beautiful unto itself, each page filled with inset images that shift, fade, or change.
> 
> Bryan Bishop, [The Verge, January 3, 2013](https://www.theverge.com/2013/1/3/3828314/the-book-of-sarth-ipad-app-graphic-novel-concept-album)

Elik Smith, credited on The Gralbum Collective’s title page as Alex Smith, is the artist of *The Book of Sarth* (2012): “Art and Story: Alex Smith,” alongside illustrations by Tom Hall and [Archie P. Valdez](/conspirators/archie-p-valdez/). [Marc Urselli](/conspirators/marc-urselli/)’s review in Chain D.L.K. described the artwork as passing through several styles, from soft cartoon images to stylised vector work, treated black-and-white photographs and, by the epilogue, pixellated, almost art deco paintings.

## Book of Sarth

He drew [Book of Sarth](/conspiracies/book-of-sarth/), my first full-length solo release, two and a half years in the making. He is the second of the four people I thanked when it was finished: Elik Smith is a genius.
