Conspirator

Gregory Cage

Guitar and laptop looping; In the Loop, with Mark Righter. I played with him, and he ran an Electrix Repeater.

Gregory Cage is a New York musician who played in In the Loop with Mark Righter, the drummer of Number19. In the Loop played the Warper Party at the Delancey on March 4, 2009, on the bill right after my set.

I played with him. He is one of the two people I played with who looped on an Electrix Repeater, the box I never used myself; the Electrix boxes I do use are the Filter Queen, the Filter Factory and the Mo-FX.

The recordWarper Party, the Delancey, 168 Delancey Street, New York, Wednesday March 4, 2009. Downstairs: 8:20 Sarth, 8:50 In The Loop, 9:20 Prez Powerz, 10:00 Moldover. The listing.

The creditGregory Cage, of In the Loop with Mark Righter, played with Sarth Calhoun; Cage looped on an Electrix Repeater. In the Loop followed Calhoun on the Warper Party bill at the Delancey, March 4, 2009.

In the Loop

In the Loop was Gregory’s band with Mark Righter, who I had formed Number19 with in 1999. The Warper Party listing is the public trace of it; the rest is mine to write.
