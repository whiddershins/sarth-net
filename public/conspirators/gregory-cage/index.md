# Gregory Cage

Guitar and laptop looping; In the Loop, with Mark Righter. I played with him, and he ran an Electrix Repeater.

Gregory Cage is a New York musician who played in In the Loop with [Mark Righter](/conspirators/mark-righter/), the drummer of [Number19](/conspiracies/number19/). In the Loop played the Warper Party at the Delancey on March 4, 2009, on the bill right after my set.

I played with him. He is one of the two people I played with who looped on an Electrix Repeater, the box I never used myself; the Electrix boxes I do use are the [Filter Queen](/devices/electrix-filter-queen/), the [Filter Factory](/devices/electrix-filter-factory/) and the [Mo-FX](/devices/electrix-mo-fx/).

The record: Warper Party, the Delancey, 168 Delancey Street, New York, Wednesday March 4, 2009. Downstairs: 8:20 Sarth, 8:50 In The Loop, 9:20 Prez Powerz, 10:00 Moldover. [The listing](https://forum.djtechtools.com/t/nyc-weds-march-4-warper-party-ableton-live-apc40-demo/28).

The credit: Gregory Cage, of In the Loop with Mark Righter, played with Sarth Calhoun; Cage looped on an Electrix Repeater. In the Loop followed Calhoun on the Warper Party bill at the Delancey, March 4, 2009.

## In the Loop

In the Loop was Gregory’s band with Mark Righter, who I had formed Number19 with in 1999. The Warper Party listing is the public trace of it; the rest is mine to write.

Who he is and the Repeater are as I said on September 26, 2026. The Warper Party bill is the organisers’ listing, posted to the DJ TechTools forum on March 2, 2009.
