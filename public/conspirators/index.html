People

Conspirators

People I have made things with.

  1. Lou ReedAmerican songwriter, guitarist and singer, 1942 to 2013, who co-founded the Velvet Underground and then made solo records for forty years. I played in his touring band and in Metal Machine Trio, and worked on both the theater score and the album of Lulu.
  2. Rob WassermanAmerican bassist, 1952 to 2016, known for the electric upright bass he developed with Ned Steinberger and for the Solo, Duets and Trios records. He and I first played together at Lou Reed’s apartment, in the session that became “Junior Dad”.
  3. Leah ColoffBrooklyn cellist, singer and songwriter. She fronts Lucibel Crater and played in Number19 with me.
  4. Paul ChuffoDrummer, originally with the punk and jazz band Gutbucket. He played in Lucibel Crater.
  5. Tony DiodoreGuitarist and violinist. He and I formed Number19 in 1999 and played together again in Lou Reed’s band from 2011.
  6. Ulrich KriegerGerman composer and saxophonist. He arranged Lou Reed’s Metal Machine Music for ensemble, and was a third of Metal Machine Trio with Reed and me.
  7. Laurie AndersonAmerican artist, composer and musician. I played between her and Lou Reed on the Yellow Pony tours in 2009, resampling their voices live.
