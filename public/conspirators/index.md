# Conspirators

People I have made things with.

- [**Lou Reed**](/conspirators/lou-reed/). American songwriter, guitarist and singer, 1942 to 2013, who co-founded the Velvet Underground and then made solo records for forty years. Sarth played in his touring band and in Metal Machine Trio, and worked on both the theater score and the album of *Lulu*.

- [**Rob Wasserman**](/conspirators/rob-wasserman/). American bassist, 1952 to 2016, known for the electric upright bass he developed with Ned Steinberger and for the *Solo*, *Duets* and *Trios* records. He and Sarth first played together at Lou Reed’s apartment, in the session that became “Junior Dad”.

- [**Leah Coloff**](/conspirators/leah-coloff/). Brooklyn cellist, singer and songwriter. She fronted Lucibel Crater and played in Number19 with Sarth.

- [**Paul Chuffo**](/conspirators/paul-chuffo/). Drummer, originally with the punk and jazz band Gutbucket. He played in Lucibel Crater.

- [**Tony Diodore**](/conspirators/tony-diodore/). Guitarist and violinist. He and Sarth formed Number19 in 1999 and played together again in Lou Reed’s band from 2011.
