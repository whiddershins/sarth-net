## Lou Reed’s bands and Metal Machine Trio

1. [![Lou Reed seated with his guitar, Metal Machine Trio on stage.](/images/mm3-nyc-wide.jpg)**Lou Reed**American songwriter, guitarist and singer, 1942 to 2013, who co-founded the Velvet Underground and then made solo records for forty years. I played in his touring band and in Metal Machine Trio, and worked on both the theater score and the album of *Lulu*.](/conspirators/lou-reed/)
2. [![Laurie Anderson at her violin and keyboard, Yellow Pony, Madrid, 2009.](/images/yellow-pony-madrid-2009.jpg)**Laurie Anderson**American artist, composer and musician. I played between her and Lou Reed on the Yellow Pony tours in 2009, resampling their voices live.](/conspirators/laurie-anderson/)
3. [![Ulrich Krieger on saxophone, Metal Machine Trio on stage.](/images/mm3-nyc-wide.jpg)**Ulrich Krieger**German composer and saxophonist. He arranged Lou Reed’s *Metal Machine Music* for ensemble, and was a third of Metal Machine Trio with Reed and me.](/conspirators/ulrich-krieger/)
4. [![Rob Wasserman on upright bass at the left of Lou Reed’s band, 2008.](/images/mwm-5113.jpg)**Rob Wasserman**American bassist, 1952 to 2016, known for the electric upright bass he developed with Ned Steinberger and for the *Solo*, *Duets* and *Trios* records. He and I first played together at Lou Reed’s apartment, in the session that became “Junior Dad”.](/conspirators/rob-wasserman/)
5. [![Kevin Hearn, 2003.](/images/conspirator-kevin-hearn.jpg)**Kevin Hearn**Barenaked Ladies keyboardist and Lou Reed’s musical director. Lou’s band with me from 2008, and the Winter Garden in 2023.](/conspirators/kevin-hearn/)
6. [![Steve Hunter with a guitar, 2013.](/images/conspirator-steve-hunter.jpg)**Steve Hunter**The guitarist of Berlin and Rock ’n’ Roll Animal. Lou Reed’s band with me in 2008 and 2009.](/conspirators/steve-hunter/)
7. [![Tony “Thunder” Smith at the drums behind Lou Reed, on an outdoor stage.](/images/conspirator-tony-thunder-smith.jpg)**Tony “Thunder” Smith**Lou Reed’s longest-serving drummer. The band with me from 2008 to 2011.](/conspirators/tony-thunder-smith/)
8. [![Aram Bajakian playing guitar at the Vancouver Jazz Festival, 2017.](/images/conspirator-aram-bajakian.jpg)**Aram Bajakian**Guitarist and composer. The eight-piece band on Lou Reed’s 2011 European tour.](/conspirators/aram-bajakian/)
9. [![Shahzad Ismaily playing bass on stage, 2009.](/images/conspirator-shahzad-ismaily.jpg)**Shahzad Ismaily**Multi-instrumentalist and owner of Figure 8 Recording. Lou Reed Drones with me in 2019 and 2023.](/conspirators/shahzad-ismaily/)
10. [![John Zorn with his saxophone at the Newport Jazz Festival, 2014.](/images/conspirator-john-zorn.jpg)**John Zorn**Composer and saxophonist. A set with Lou’s band in 2008, sitting in with Metal Machine Trio in 2009, and the drones in 2019.](/conspirators/john-zorn/)
11. [![Marc Ribot playing guitar on stage, 2007.](/images/conspirator-marc-ribot.jpg)**Marc Ribot**Guitarist. The ten-piece set at Noise Night, Sydney Opera House, 2010.](/conspirators/marc-ribot/)
12. [**Mike Rathke**Lou Reed’s guitarist since New York. The 2008 band, a trio at the Whitney, and “Solsbury Hill.”](/conspirators/mike-rathke/)
13. [**Stewart Hurwood**Lou Reed’s guitar tech, who performs Lou Reed Drones. I have played inside the drones in 2019 and 2023.](/conspirators/stewart-hurwood/)
