# Conspirators

People I have made things with.

1. [**Lou Reed**American songwriter, guitarist and singer, 1942 to 2013, who co-founded the Velvet Underground and then made solo records for forty years. I played in his touring band and in Metal Machine Trio, and worked on both the theater score and the album of *Lulu*.](/conspirators/lou-reed/)
2. [**Rob Wasserman**American bassist, 1952 to 2016, known for the electric upright bass he developed with Ned Steinberger and for the *Solo*, *Duets* and *Trios* records. He and I first played together at Lou Reed’s apartment, in the session that became “Junior Dad”.](/conspirators/rob-wasserman/)
3. [**Leah Coloff**Brooklyn cellist, singer and songwriter. She fronts Lucibel Crater and played in Number19 with me.](/conspirators/leah-coloff/)
4. [**Paul Chuffo**Drummer, originally with the punk and jazz band Gutbucket. He played in Lucibel Crater.](/conspirators/paul-chuffo/)
5. [**Tony Diodore**Guitarist and violinist. He and I formed Number19 in 1999 and played together again in Lou Reed’s band from 2011.](/conspirators/tony-diodore/)
6. [**Ulrich Krieger**German composer and saxophonist. He arranged Lou Reed’s *Metal Machine Music* for ensemble, and was a third of Metal Machine Trio with Reed and me.](/conspirators/ulrich-krieger/)
7. [**Laurie Anderson**American artist, composer and musician. I played between her and Lou Reed on the Yellow Pony tours in 2009, resampling their voices live.](/conspirators/laurie-anderson/)
