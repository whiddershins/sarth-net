# Jacob McCoy

Filmmaker, and Director, Production at Third Wall Studio. We have made things together since Book of Sarth.

[Jacob McCoy](https://jacobmccoy.com/) is a filmmaker and visual artist working across documentary, music video and site-specific installation, and Director, Production at [Third Wall Studio](https://www.thirdwallstudio.com/). He directed, shot and edited the documentary on *The Gauntlet*, the choral and movement work by [Sxip Shirey](/conspirators/sxip-shirey/) and Coco Karol, streamed by the Sydney Opera House in 2020, and made the archival film for their *We Don’t Need the Sea to Drown* at National Sawdust in 2025 and 2026. He plays Linnstrument and bass.

## From Book of Sarth to Third Wall

He was one of the four people I thanked when [Book of Sarth](/conspiracies/book-of-sarth/) was finished in 2012: Jacob McCoy is unstoppable. On March 2, 2015, Lou Reed’s birthday, we recorded Linnstrument and Continuum duets; one became “[For Lovers](/transmissions/singles/),” dedicated to Lou, filmed that spring and released as a single in 2020. He plays the Linnstrument drones on the second [Introspections](/transmissions/introspections/) video, filmed and edited the two duets with [Sxip Shirey](/conspirators/sxip-shirey/) that same year, and plays bass on “[I Just Believe in Christmas](/conspiracies/i-just-believe-in-christmas/).”

At [Third Wall Studio](/conspiracies/third-wall-studio/) he is Director, Production, and he directed three of the studio’s films, all made in [Burlap](/conspiracies/burlap/): *Meatsuitz – Dare to be Delicious*, a meat-themed swimwear ad, December 2025; *Meatsuitz Family Vacay*, January 2026; and *Through the Brambles*, the studio’s 360° VR film, May 2026.
