# Jonathan Arons

The dancing trombonist, artist in residence at Third Wall Studio. *The Trinary Matrix* is his film.

[Jonathan Arons](https://www.jonarons.com/) is a New York trombonist, dancer and entertainer known as the dancing trombonist, a member of the Chops Horns who has backed Alicia Keys, Aretha Franklin, Stevie Wonder and Mariah Carey and played in the *Dancing with the Stars* orchestra under Ray Chew; he played on the Dave Holland Big Band’s Grammy-winning *Overtime* (2005). He competed on *America’s Got Talent* in 2008 and 2025, holds degrees in jazz trombone and biochemistry from Oberlin, and wrote the memoir *Novelty Act* and *The Trinary Matrix*, a prequel to *The Matrix*. He tours with Gloria Gaynor.

## Third Wall Studio

He is an artist in residence at [Third Wall Studio](/conspiracies/third-wall-studio/), and *The Trinary Matrix*, from his book, is his film there, one of the 2026 shorts made in [Burlap](/conspiracies/burlap/).
