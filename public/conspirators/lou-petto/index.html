Lou Petto

Drummer. Our one-take duet is “Softly Questioning.”

Here is one of my favorite tracks from it, called "Softly Questioning." It was improvised and recorded in one take, with no editing. A duet with continuum fingerboard and Lou Petto on drums.

Sarth Calhoun, sarth.net, November 29, 2015

Lou Petto is a drummer who lives and works in the New Jersey and New York City area, playing across genres, composing, and making live and studio soundscapes for film. He drums on “Softly Questioning,” the third track of Reflections, Vol. 1 (November 2015).

Softly Questioning

“Softly Questioning,” the third track of Reflections, Vol. 1, is a duet for Continuum Fingerboard and his drums, improvised and recorded in one take with no editing. Its melody became the main theme of Joe Kelly’s film Poughkeepsie, and I returned to it as “Softly Questioning (Reprise)” in 2025.
