# Mark Righter

Drummer of Number19, and a furniture maker.

[Mark Righter](https://cambiumstudio.com/mark-righter) is a drummer, percussionist and vocalist from New Haven, Connecticut, living in Brooklyn, and a founding member of the Harlem collective Casa Mantequilla. In 2004 he founded Cambium Studio, a furniture design and build firm now in the Brooklyn Navy Yard, and he teaches part-time at Parsons School of Design.

## Number19

He played drums in [Number19](/conspiracies/number19/), the band [Tony Diodore](/conspirators/tony-diodore/), [Leah Coloff](/conspirators/leah-coloff/) and I formed in Brooklyn in 1999, and on its one album, [*Suspension*](/transmissions/suspension/) (2001), which the band paid for out of mp3.com downloads.
