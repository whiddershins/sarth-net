# Michael Patrick Flanagan Smith

Folksinger, playwright and writer. He sings “I Just Believe in Christmas.”

[Michael Patrick F. Smith](https://www.thegoodhand.org/) is a folksinger, playwright and writer from Frederick, Maryland. He ran Baltimore’s Living Room Company from 1999 to 2005 and moved to New York in 2011 to stage his play *Woody Guthrie Dreams* at Theater for the New City. As a musician he records and performs as The Good Hand. His first book, *The Good Hand: A Memoir of Work, Brotherhood, and Transformation in an American Boomtown* (Viking, 2021), recounts his time as an oil field hand in Williston, North Dakota; he testified to a U.S. House subcommittee in May 2021, and his reporting has appeared in The New York Times, The Washington Post Magazine and The Guardian. He lives in central Kentucky.

## The Christmas song and the podcast

The idea for “[I Just Believe in Christmas](/conspiracies/i-just-believe-in-christmas/)” came out of a Christmas Eve 2014 email exchange with my Brooklyn neighbors, and I immediately thought it should be sung by him. We recorded it at Figure 8 in August 2015 and released it that December as Catfish Calhoun and Friends; his acoustic demo is on my YouTube channel. On February 15, 2021 he was the guest on the sixth episode of [Beautiful Tornado](/transmissions/beautiful-tornado/), “The Re-Enchantment of the World,” and played his covid-era songs live on the show.
