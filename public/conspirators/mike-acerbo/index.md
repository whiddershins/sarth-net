# Mike Acerbo

Singer-songwriter. I played bass on “Trilby,” the opening track of *The Search*.

> Mike did a wonderful job not only writing it but arranging it, singing it, playing guitar on it, and generally making it happen. As he should, since it is his thing. Go Mike!
> 
> Sarth Calhoun, [sarth.net, January 14, 2012](https://web.archive.org/web/20140905181543/http://www.sarth.net/category/conspiracies/mike-acerbo/)

[Mike Acerbo](https://mikeacerbo.bandcamp.com/) is a singer-songwriter born in the Bronx and raised in Pearl River, New York. His debut album *The Search*, recorded over about ten years from 2001, was released on February 14, 2012; its opening track is “Trilby.” A second album, *Dark Place*, recorded at Sear Sound and MonoLisa Studios with [Leah Coloff](/conspirators/leah-coloff/) on cello and vocals and [Rich Kulsar](/conspirators/rich-kulsar/) on drums, followed in July 2026.

## Trilby

I played bass on “Trilby.” The first parts were recorded about a decade before the record came out, and I rediscovered my playing at the video release party in January 2012. On my old site the record was a conspiracy of its own.
