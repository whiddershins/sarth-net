Conspirators / Mike Rathke

Conspirator

Mike Rathke

Lou Reed’s guitarist since New York. We played together in Lou’s band, as a trio at the Whitney, and on “Solsbury Hill.”

Reed performed an amazing set including ‘Dirty Blvd.’ and ‘Who Am I’ with Mike Rathke on guitar and Sarth Calhoun on electronic processing.

loureed.com, June 25, 2009, on the Whitney Museum opening

Mike Rathke is an American guitarist who was Lou Reed’s principal guitarist from the New York album (1989), on which he plays guitar and shares the mixing credit, and who co-wrote five songs on Magic and Loss (1992) with Reed; his credits run through Perfect Night: Live in London (1998) and Animal Serenade (2004). In a PBS American Masters interview he recalls meeting Reed the day after his guitars were stolen, when Reed handed him a guitar, and about ten years of writing music to Reed’s lyrics.

Lou Reed’s band, a trio, and a record

He was one of the two guitarists, with Steve Hunter, in the band around Lou when I joined for the spring 2008 U.S. tour, and at Lollapalooza 2009. On June 23, 2009 the three of us played the opening of the Persol exhibition at the Whitney Museum of American Art as a trio: Lou, Mike on guitar, and me on electronic processing. We share a credit on Lou’s recording of Peter Gabriel’s “Solsbury Hill”: additional guitar by him, additional programming by me.
