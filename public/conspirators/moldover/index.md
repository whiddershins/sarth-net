# Moldover

Musician and instrument designer, the coiner of controllerism. We shared Warper Party bills in New York and have improvised together.

Matthew [Moldover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moldover) (born 1980), known as Moldover, is a musician, technologist and instrument designer based in San Francisco. He studied electronic music composition at Berklee, moved to New York to play in bands, and in 2005 coined the term controllerism for making music live with computer controllers and software; the MIDI Association has called him the Godfather of Controllerism. His instruments include the Mojo, the Robocaster, the Guitar Wing and a line of multiplayer Jamboxes, and he has performed at Sónar, Mutek and Coachella.

## Warper Party and after

We were on the same bill at the Warper Party at The Delancey in New York on March 4, 2009, and Clocktower’s Warper Party Radio Extravaganza lists us both among its performers. On July 9, 2012 I posted about his Jamboxes on my old site, under [Contraptions](/contraptions/); only the title survives. I have named him among the improvisers I explored the [Introspections](/transmissions/introspections/) ideas with, and my [CV](/work/) lists a video with him.
