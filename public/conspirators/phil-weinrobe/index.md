# Phil Weinrobe

Producer and engineer. He recorded “I Just Believe in Christmas” at Figure 8 and played banjo on it.

From the credits: Phil Weinrobe, banjo. Recorded by Phil Weinrobe at Figure 8 Studios. Mixed by Marc Urselli. Mastered by Fred Kevorkian. *I Just Believe in Christmas*, 2015.

[Philip Weinrobe](https://www.philipweinrobe.com/) is a producer, recording engineer and mix engineer in Brooklyn. He helped build Figure 8 Recording in Prospect Heights with [Shahzad Ismaily](/conspirators/shahzad-ismaily/) and Eli Crews, engineering there until 2020, when he started his own studio, Sugar Mountain. His credits include Adrianne Lenker’s *Songs*, *Instrumentals* and *Bright Future*, nominated for Best Folk Album at the 2025 Grammys, and records by Cass McCombs, Tomberlin, Buck Meek and Alanis Morissette.

## I Just Believe in Christmas

He recorded “[I Just Believe in Christmas](/conspiracies/i-just-believe-in-christmas/)” at Figure 8 in August 2015 and played banjo on it. [Marc Urselli](/conspirators/marc-urselli/) mixed it.
