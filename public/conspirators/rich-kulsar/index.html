Conspirator

Rich Kulsar

Drummer and Grammy winner. We are both on Mike Acerbo’s The Search.

You know who else is really featured on this record (besides Mike A) is Rich Kulsar and Leah Coloff … two great musicians who I get never enough chance to work with.

Sarth Calhoun, sarth.net, January 14, 2012

Rich Kulsar is a drummer and percussionist working in New York since 1991, and co-owner of MonoLisa Studios in Long Island City. He won a Grammy in 2019 for Best Children’s Album as a member of Lucy Kalantari & the Jazz Cats, played drums on Leah Coloff’s debut Dark Sweet Heart (1999) and on Mike Acerbo’s records, and his credits include Roger Waters, Micky Dolenz, Bruce Cockburn and Blood, Sweat & Tears.

The Search

He drums on Mike Acerbo’s The Search (2012), the record whose opening track, “Trilby,” has my bass on it. My old WordPress site had a conspirator page for him.
