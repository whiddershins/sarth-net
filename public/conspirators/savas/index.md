# Savas

Designer, creative director and artist, Head of Creation at Third Wall Studio. *Kubler-Ross Model* is his film.

[Savaş Özay](https://savasozay.com/), credited at Third Wall Studio as Savas, is a designer, creative director and artist based in Amsterdam with over twenty years of practice across identity, product design, art direction and image-making, for clients in fashion, sport, automotive and electronic music. He is Head of Creation at [Third Wall Studio](https://www.thirdwallstudio.com/).

[Embedded player](https://player.vimeo.com/video/1187052948?h=25ddad82d9)

> Savas brought us a world the generative tools had no idea what to do with. Creatures, environments, a visual language outside any reference set. The default approach failed early. So we worked backward from his art direction and broke the world into parts the tools could actually hold: figures, plates, gestures, atmosphere. We composited those parts back into shots that kept his hand visible in every frame.
> 
> Third Wall Studio, [Process](https://www.thirdwallstudio.com/process)
