Conspirator

Sxip Shirey

Composer, performer and storyteller. We recorded two improvised duets in 2015, his shruti box and prepared guitar against my Continuum.

Gene “Sxip” Shirey is an American composer, performer and storyteller who grew up in Athens, Ohio, and moved to New York in the late 1980s. He works with found objects, rare and modified instruments, and electronics. He was a founding member of Luminescent Orchestrii, released the solo albums Sombule (2006) and Sonic New York (2010), and scored Neil Gaiman’s short film Statuesque (2009).

He was composer and music director of the circus production LIMBO, which toured from 2013 to 2019, and with the choreographer Coco Karol he created The Gauntlet, a site-specific choral and movement work first performed at National Sawdust in 2018 and since at the Sydney Opera House, Rockefeller Center and the Cleveland Museum of Art. He is a 2011 United States Artists Fellow and a curator and artist in residence at National Sawdust in Brooklyn.

Sxip Shirey visited (Gr)album World Headquarters last week with an idea for a really cool piece. This is based on his Shruti Box, which is a traditional Indian drone instrument working on bellows, similar to a harmonium ... or similar to an accordion.

Sarth Calhoun, YouTube, May 29, 2015

Introspections

In 2015 he came to (Gr)album World Headquarters and we recorded improvised duets, released on my YouTube channel: “Continuum Fingerboard vs. Shruti Box” on May 29, 2015, and “Prepared Guitar and Continuum Fingerboard” on June 30, 2015. In the first, he creates the drones and structure with an amplified shruti box while I play Continuum. Both are part of Introspections, and I have named him among the improvisers I explored those ideas with, alongside Laurie Anderson, Moldover, John Zorn, Marc Ribot and Rob Wasserman.

Jacob McCoy, who plays the Linnstrument drones on the other Introspections duet, directed, shot and edited the documentary on The Gauntlet.
