Tetiana Khodakivska

Film director and editor, artist in residence at Third Wall Studio.

Tetiana Khodakivska (born 1980, Kyiv) is a Ukrainian film director and editor based in New York since 2015, a member of the European Film Academy and the Ukrainian Film Academy. She won the Chicago International Film Festival Gold Plaque for the series Angels of War and the Golden Dzyga for editing Foxter & Max, and is a Sundance Institute grantee developing The Blue Sweater with a Yellow Hole, an animated documentary in which Ukrainian children returned from Russian “re-education” camps paint their memories.

Third Wall Studio

She is an artist in residence at Third Wall Studio, developing The Blue Sweater with a Yellow Hole, which spans four countries and three studios.
