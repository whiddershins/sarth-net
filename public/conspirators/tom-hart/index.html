Conspirators / Tom Hart

Conspirator

Tom Hart

The artist of Train O’ Thots, drawn on the subway.

We are excited to launch the Train O' Thots app on April 1st, 2015 with a special showing at the New York Transit Museum.

Sarth Calhoun, sarth.net, March 28, 2015

Tom Hart is a New York artist who draws annotated portraits of subway riders during his commutes, by hand with fountain pen, brush and cut-paper collage, adding invented biographical text with “names changed to protect the peculiar.” Stereophile, at the Gralbum launch, saw NYC bums, metal-heads, and hipsters on the subway with gruff but comical drawings and incisive poetry.

Two gralbums

The Gralbum Collective published Train O’ Thots in April 2014, his drawings and words with music by Moon Hooch, and Train O’ Thoughts in April 2015, with music by Exaltron (Scott Hampton) and me: “Hope is a state of Mind” is mine. The second launched with a showing at the New York Transit Museum on April 1, 2015. Video and animation on both were by Jacob McCoy and Leah Coloff.

The creditThe Gralbum Collective, founded by Sarth Calhoun, published Tom Hart’s Train O’ Thots (2014, music by Moon Hooch) and Train O’ Thoughts (2015, music by Exaltron and Calhoun).
