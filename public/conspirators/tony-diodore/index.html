Conspirator

Tony Diodore

Tony Diodore is a guitarist and violinist in Brooklyn. He has played in Number19, Puracane and BM LINX. From 2011 he played guitar and violin in Lou Reed’s touring band, the same band Sarth Calhoun was already in.

Number19

Diodore, Sarth, Leah Coloff and Marcus Righter formed Number19 in 1999, with Tony on guitar and violin. The band funded its first record, Suspension, out of mp3.com downloads, at one point sitting at number eighteen overall on the site.

Puracane and BM LINX

After Number19, Diodore played guitar and violin on Puracane’s I’ve Been Here the Longest and co-wrote “Watershed.” In 2005 he formed BM LINX with Jonathan Murray. That band released The Portable Genius in 2006 and Black Entertainment in 2008, toured the United States, Canada and Europe, and went on hiatus in 2010.

Back in the same band

A tour announcement on 23 May 2011 named Diodore as Lou Reed’s newly confirmed guitarist. He toured Europe that summer in Lou’s band on guitar and violin, and returned in 2012 for the From VU to Lulu European cycle, where Copenhagen, Bonn and Bordeaux coverage all name him. He also played the Wrecking Ball gala at St. Ann’s Warehouse with Lou on 7 May 2012. The dates are on Appearances and on the page for the 2011 European tour.

Diodore later described Sarth’s job in that band as “Lou’s imagination.” When Lou wanted a wild sound, or something entirely outside the band’s normal vocabulary, Sarth was the one who supplied it.

How Sarth introduced him

Tony, Sarth, Leah Coloff, and Marcus Righter formed Number19 in 1999. After we released Suspension, Tony went on to play with Puracane, and eventually form BM Linx. Now, Tony and Sarth have joined forces again, playing with Lou Reed.

Sarth wrote that introduction for the conspirators section of his old WordPress site, recovered from the Wayback Machine.
