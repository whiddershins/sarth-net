Basses

My first instrument. A 1974 Fender Jazz Bass, a Warwick six-string, and others.

Bass is my first instrument. In my teens I stopped buying comic books to save up for my first bass guitar. I have a 1974 Fender Jazz Bass and a Warwick six-string bass, and other basses.

Number19: bass and keys. Lucibel Crater: keys, bass, loops. “I Before E” is the slap-bass track, an homage to Robert Trujillo. On Lou Reed’s “Peggy Sue” the bass is a Moog Little Phatty, which is on the Moog page.

Yes, I have a 1974 Fender Jazz Bass and a Warwick six-string bass. These are both really amazing. I’ve got other basses as well.

Sarth Calhoun, September 26, 2026

The creditSarth Calhoun, bass: Number19 (Suspension, 2001, bass and Yamaha EX5), Lucibel Crater (2005 to 2008, keys, bass and loops), and Lou Reed’s “Peggy Sue” (2011, a Moog Little Phatty). A 1974 Fender Jazz Bass and a Warwick six-string, among others.

Suspension, 2001, bass and a Yamaha EX5. Miracles, 2007, and The Family Album, 2008, with Lucibel Crater. The note I wrote about learning bass, Rob Wasserman, John Paul Jones and Robert Trujillo is on the I Before E page.
