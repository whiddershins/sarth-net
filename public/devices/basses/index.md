# Basses

My first instrument. A 1974 Fender Jazz Bass, a Warwick six-string, and others.

Bass is my first instrument. In my teens I stopped buying comic books to save up for my first bass guitar. I have a 1974 [Fender Jazz Bass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fender_Jazz_Bass) and a [Warwick](https://www.warwick.de/) six-string bass, and other basses.

[Number19](/conspiracies/number19/): bass and keys. [Lucibel Crater](/conspiracies/lucibel-crater/): keys, bass, loops. [“I Before E”](/transmissions/i-before-e/) is the slap-bass track, an homage to Robert Trujillo. On Lou Reed’s [“Peggy Sue”](/transmissions/peggy-sue/) the bass is a Moog Little Phatty, which is on the [Moog](/devices/moog/) page.

> Yes, I have a 1974 Fender Jazz Bass and a Warwick six-string bass. These are both really amazing. I’ve got other basses as well.
> 
> Sarth Calhoun, September 26, 2026

The credit: Sarth Calhoun, bass: Number19 (*Suspension*, 2001, bass and Yamaha EX5), Lucibel Crater (2005 to 2008, keys, bass and loops), and Lou Reed’s “Peggy Sue” (2011, a Moog Little Phatty). A 1974 Fender Jazz Bass and a Warwick six-string, among others.
