# Haken Continuum Fingerboard

A surface that tracks the fingers in three dimensions: continuous pitch, not keys. My instrument since Lucibel Crater, in Lou Reed’s band, Metal Machine Trio, Yellow Pony and *Lulu*.

The Continuum Fingerboard is a long neoprene playing surface that tracks finger position and pressure in three dimensions. Continuous pitch, not keys. Full-size or half-size, and often driving [Kyma](/devices/kyma/). Maker: [Lippold Haken](/conspirators/lippold-haken/), Haken Audio, Champaign, Illinois. [hakenaudio.com](https://www.hakenaudio.com/)

It has been my instrument since [Lucibel Crater](/conspiracies/lucibel-crater/) and through the Reed years. In [Metal Machine Trio](/conspiracies/metal-machine-trio/), Lou and I both had Continuums onstage. [Yellow Pony](/conspiracies/yellow-pony/): Continuum plus live resampling of Reed and Anderson’s voices. [*Lulu*](/conspiracies/lulu/): dual Continuum improvisations with a live string section. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuum_Fingerboard) names me as an advocate.

## Conspiracies

The Continuum is on [Lou Reed Live at Lollapalooza 2009](/transmissions/lou-reed-live-at-lollapalooza-2009/), credited “electronics & continuum,” across [Lou Reed’s song band](/conspiracies/lou-reed-song-band/), [Metal Machine Trio](/conspiracies/metal-machine-trio/), [Yellow Pony](/conspiracies/yellow-pony/) and [*Lulu*](/conspiracies/lulu/), on [Introspections](/transmissions/introspections/), and on the [Lucibel Crater](/conspiracies/lucibel-crater/) records. Symbolic Sound’s post [Walking the road that only you can see](https://news.symbolicsound.com/2012/10/walking-the-road-that-only-you-can-see/) is one take of Continuum, Kyma and Ableton amp modelers. The photograph of me at it is on [About](/about/).
