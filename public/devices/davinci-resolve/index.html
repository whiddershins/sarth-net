DaVinci Resolve

Blackmagic Design’s edit, colour and audio application. Where Through the Brambles was cut and its ambisonic mix finished.

DaVinci Resolve is Blackmagic Design’s application for editing, colour grading, visual effects and audio post-production. Maker: Blackmagic Design, Melbourne. blackmagicdesign.com

At Third Wall Studio the edit and the ambisonic mix of Through the Brambles, the 360° VR film, were finished in Resolve, after the stills and motion were made in Burlap.
