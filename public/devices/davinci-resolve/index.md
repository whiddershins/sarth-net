# DaVinci Resolve

Blackmagic Design’s edit, colour and audio application. Where *Through the Brambles* was cut and its ambisonic mix finished.

[DaVinci Resolve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DaVinci_Resolve) is Blackmagic Design’s application for editing, colour grading, visual effects and audio post-production. Maker: Blackmagic Design, Melbourne. [blackmagicdesign.com](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve)

At [Third Wall Studio](/conspiracies/third-wall-studio/) the edit and the ambisonic mix of [*Through the Brambles*](/transmissions/through-the-brambles/), the 360° VR film, were finished in Resolve, after the stills and motion were made in [Burlap](/conspiracies/burlap/).
