Electrix Mo-FX

Tremolo, flanger, filter and delay with tempo sync, from the Electrix line. On my table now.

The Mo-FX is the multi-effect in the Electrix line, the performance boxes IVL Technologies made in Victoria, British Columbia, around 1999 to 2002: tremolo, flanger, filter and delay, each with tempo sync, on a front panel of knobs.

I use three of the Electrix boxes now: the Mo-FX, and the Filter Queen and Filter Factory. I never used the Repeater, the looper in the line; two people I played with did, Brandt Passalacqua and Gregory Cage.

The lineThe Electrix performance line, from IVL Technologies in Victoria, British Columbia, around 1999 to 2002: the Repeater, a four-track loop sampler; the Filter Queen, a half-rack analogue filter; the Filter Factory, a full-size stereo analogue filter; the Warp Factory, a vocoder; the Mo-FX, a tempo-synced multi-effect; the EQ Killer, a three-band isolator. The line was discontinued in 2002.

All three nowadays, and I have a lot to say about them.

Sarth Calhoun, September 26, 2026, on the Filter Queen, the Filter Factory and the Mo-FX
