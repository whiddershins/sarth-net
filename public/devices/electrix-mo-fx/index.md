# Electrix Mo-FX

Tremolo, flanger, filter and delay with tempo sync, from the Electrix line. On my table now.

The Mo-FX is the multi-effect in the Electrix line, the performance boxes IVL Technologies made in Victoria, British Columbia, around 1999 to 2002: tremolo, flanger, filter and delay, each with tempo sync, on a front panel of knobs.

I use three of the Electrix boxes now: the Mo-FX, and the [Filter Queen](/devices/electrix-filter-queen/) and [Filter Factory](/devices/electrix-filter-factory/). I never used the Repeater, the looper in the line; two people I played with did, [Brandt Passalacqua](/conspirators/brandt-passalacqua/) and [Gregory Cage](/conspirators/gregory-cage/).

The line: The Electrix performance line, from IVL Technologies in Victoria, British Columbia, around 1999 to 2002: the [Repeater](https://www.vintagesynth.com/electrix-pro/repeater), a four-track loop sampler; the Filter Queen, a half-rack analogue filter; the Filter Factory, a full-size stereo analogue filter; the Warp Factory, a vocoder; the Mo-FX, a tempo-synced multi-effect; the EQ Killer, a three-band isolator. The line was discontinued in 2002.

> All three nowadays, and I have a lot to say about them.
> 
> Sarth Calhoun, September 26, 2026, on the Filter Queen, the Filter Factory and the Mo-FX
