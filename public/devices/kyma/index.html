Device · Machine

Symbolic Sound Kyma

A graphical sound-design environment and the Paca, the orange box that computes it. Under Metal Machine Trio, the Reed tours, Lulu, Lucibel Crater and The Book of Sarth.

Kyma is a graphical sound-design environment. The Paca is dedicated DSP hardware, a small orange box, rack-mountable, not a plugin inside a laptop; the Pacarana is its larger sibling. Maker: Symbolic Sound Corporation, Carla Scaletti and Kurt Hebel, Champaign, Illinois. kyma.symbolicsound.com

I beta-tested Kyma and am credited on the Kyma X startup screen. Lucibel Crater ran its processing through it. Metal Machine Trio: live Kyma processing of the band. The 2011 Lou Reed European tour: multiple rack-mounted Pacas. Lulu at the Berliner Ensemble: Kyma processing and Continuum/Kyma playing. The Book of Sarth score is Kyma-drenched. I built SeqOSC in it, a new kind of synthesis, a relative of AM synthesis derived from exploring the analogies between rhythm and timbre. Junior Dad: Rob Wasserman’s electric upright through Kyma, first session at Lou’s apartment.

Symbolic Sound’s postsLou Reed’s Euro tour, July 2011: the rack of Pacas. Walking the road that only you can see, October 2012: one take, Continuum and Kyma. The Book of Sarth, November 2012.

The creditSarth Calhoun and Kyma: beta tester, credited on the Kyma X startup screen; live Kyma processing in Metal Machine Trio, on Lou Reed’s 2008 to 2012 tours with rack-mounted Pacas, on Lulu at the Berliner Ensemble and across The Book of Sarth; SeqOSC, his synthesis, built in Kyma; Rob Wasserman’s bass through Kyma on “Junior Dad.”
