# Moog

The Little Phatty on “Peggy Sue,” Lou Reed’s Moog on “Cheat on Me,” and the pedals I brought back from the factory.

[Moog Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moog_Music) makes analogue synthesizers and effects in Asheville, North Carolina, the company Bob Moog founded. The Little Phatty, from 2006, and the Minimoog Voyager, from 2002, are two of its keyboards. [moogmusic.com](https://www.moogmusic.com/)

On [Lou Reed](/conspirators/lou-reed/)’s [“Peggy Sue”](/transmissions/peggy-sue/), 2011, the bass is a Moog Little Phatty. On the [*Lulu*](/conspiracies/lulu/) sessions with Metallica, the first song we recorded was “Cheat on Me,” which opens on a riff I play alone on the Moog; that Moog was Lou’s, a Minimoog Voyager. Lou brought the whole band to the Moog factory, and I brought some Moog pedals back from there.

> The Moog that I played on “Cheat On Me” was Lou Reed’s Moog. It was a Mini-Moog Voyager, I believe, before Lou brought the whole band to the Moog factory. They call it Moog, but anyway, it was pretty great to see them making this stuff there. How few people were able to do all the output at that company. And I brought back some pedals from there. I’ve always loved those pedals; they’re amazing. The knobs are great.
> 
> Sarth Calhoun, September 26, 2026

The credit: Sarth Calhoun and Moog: a Little Phatty on Lou Reed’s “Peggy Sue” (*Rave On Buddy Holly*, 2011, Discogs credit), and Lou Reed’s Minimoog Voyager on the *Lulu* recording of “Cheat on Me” (2011); Moog pedals since the band’s visit to the factory.

## On the record

The Discogs credit for [“Peggy Sue”](/transmissions/peggy-sue/) reads bass, Little Phatty. The first day of the *Lulu* recording, and the riff on the Moog, are in the note on the [I Before E](/transmissions/i-before-e/) page.

The Little Phatty is the Discogs credit. Lou’s Voyager, the factory and the pedals are as I said on September 26, 2026, the Voyager confirmed the same day, and in my note on I Before E.
