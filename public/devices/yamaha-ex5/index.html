Device · Machine

Yamaha EX5

Yamaha’s 1998 workstation synthesizer. The keyboard on Suspension.

The Yamaha EX5 is a 76-key workstation synthesizer Yamaha released in 1998, with sampling, analogue-modelling and physical-modelling engines in one instrument. Maker: Yamaha Corporation, Hamamatsu.

It was my keyboard in Number19. On Suspension, 2001, I played bass and the EX5; Tony Diodore was on guitar and violin, Leah Coloff on cello and voice, Mark Righter on drums.

The creditSarth Calhoun, bass and Yamaha EX5, on Suspension by Number19, Searching Eye Records, 2001.
