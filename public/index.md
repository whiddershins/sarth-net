Burlap

[Burlap](/conspiracies/burlap/) is the canvas underneath it all: a native app for visual creation and context management. Connect it to the latest models from OpenAI, Google, Runway, ByteDance, and more. When using your own API keys, your data never touches our server. [Download for Mac](https://burlap.app/download) · [burlap.app](https://burlap.app) · [Contraptions](/contraptions/)

Earlier

Before the studio and Burlap: Number19 with Leah Coloff, Tony Diodore, and Mark Righter; Lucibel Crater with Coloff and Paul Chuffo; and [Book of Sarth](https://bookofsarth.com), the first gralbum. With [Lou Reed](/conspirators/lou-reed/) and [Ulrich Krieger](/conspirators/ulrich-krieger/), I made [Metal Machine Trio](/conspiracies/metal-machine-trio/) and recorded *The Creation of the Universe* over two nights at REDCAT. Lou and I later wrote the original [*Lulu* score](/conspiracies/lulu/) for [Robert Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Wilson_(director))’s [Berliner Ensemble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berliner_Ensemble) production; the [Metallica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metallica) album grew from those recordings. [Junior Dad](/conspiracies/junior-dad/) began in Lou’s apartment, with [Rob Wasserman](/conspirators/rob-wasserman/)’s electric upright running through Kyma. I still use Kyma and the Haken Continuum.
