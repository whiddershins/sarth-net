# Intrigue

Intrigue was the news page of the Squarespace sarth.net, from 2015 until this site replaced it in 2026. It carried the Rumors posts, newest first, and a column of conspiracies. Each post is on this site now, at the link, and its old address under /rumors/ serves it. Its sister blog was [The transmissions have begun . . .](/words/)
