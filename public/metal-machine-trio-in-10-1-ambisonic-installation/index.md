# Metal Machine Trio in 10.1 Ambisonic Installation

The MM3 Blender Theater shows have been reborn as a[10.1 ambisonic installation](https://web.archive.org/web/20120131143544/http://www.csulb.edu:80/org/uam/EXHIBITIONSupcoming.html) hosted by California State University at Long Beach (that’s csulb for those in the know) and I do wonder what it sounds like!

Lou and Ulrich both say it sounds amazing so it must be true.

It utilizes some mad technology by the guys at Arup Engineering SoundLab. Oh yeah. They had mics all over that place, and now they present the music to you as if you are there on stage, or something.

More details to come?

Maybe!

The post ended with the exhibition’s photo from the CSULB site, titled “MM3 with secret guy in the background”. It was not recovered.
