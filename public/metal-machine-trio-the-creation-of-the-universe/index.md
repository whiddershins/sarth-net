# Metal Machine Trio “The Creation of the Universe”

**This is the old page, kept as it was.** Today’s page: [The Creation of the Universe](/transmissions/the-creation-of-the-universe/).

![Lou Reed's Metal Machine Trio - The Creation of the Universe](/images/art-creation-of-the-universe.jpg)

Our first release is a live recording of 2 great nights live at the REDCAT in L.A. THE CREATION OF THE UNIVERSE.

These two special live concert recordings of non-vocal music featuring Lou on guitar and electronics, Ulrich Krieger on tenor sax and live-electronics, and Sarth Calhoun on live processing and Fingerboard Continuum.
