# Photos of Noise Night, Sydney

A two photo gallery, first published at this address on July 10, 2010. The post had no other text. [Noise Night](/conspiracies/vivid-live/) was May 31, 2010, in the Opera Theatre at the Sydney Opera House, during [Vivid LIVE 2010](/conspiracies/vivid-live/).

## The photos, as captioned

1. **Noise Night at the Opera House.** Alt text: “Noise Night”. File: L9999054.jpeg.
2. **Sydney Noise Night.** Alt text: “Sarth, Marc Ribot, Lou Reed, Justice Yeldham, Ichirou Agata, Laurie Anderson”. File: L9999097-Edit-3.jpeg.

The image files were not recovered. The Internet Archive holds only the October 23, 2012 capture of the thumbnail, which by then answered with the host’s suspended account page, and no copy has turned up anywhere else.
