# Power and Serenity

Lou Reed put Power and Serenity up for sale on his site. Awesome, glad to see this project reach fruition.

[http://loureed.com/power/](https://web.archive.org/web/2010/http://loureed.com/power/)

It is interesting to me to reflect on the notion that this meditation music was the first thing I ever worked on with Lou. Really this was what started our collaborations, and I have always loved the music on this. It is in a similar vein, but of a different character, than the Creation of the Universe or Hudson Wind Meditations. The compositions were conceived of, improvised upon, reworked, edited, remixed and transformed over a period of 4 years. I hope taiji enthusiasts are drawn to this release … CD version of the unabridged music coming soon!
