(re) Discovered some old bass playing

And a great track, too.

Mike Acerbo was just written up in CMJ for his new record, which reminds me that I went to the video release celebration last night and (re) discovered I played bass on that track! Blast from the past … it’s probably been a decade since we recorded some of those initial parts but the song is good as ever and Mike did a wonderful job not only writing it but arranging it, singing it, playing guitar on it, and generally making it happen.

As he should, since it is his thing. Go Mike!

Trilby by CMJ Network

You know who else is really featured on this record (besides Mike A) is Rich Kulsar and Leah Coloff … two great musicians who I get never enough chance to work with.
