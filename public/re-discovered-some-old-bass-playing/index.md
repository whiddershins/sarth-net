# (re) Discovered some old bass playing

And a great track, too.

[Mike Acerbo](http://www.mikeacerbo.com/home.html) was just [written up in CMJ](https://web.archive.org/web/20120303062942/http://www.cmj.com/news/cmj-exclusive-mike-acerbo-premieres-trilby/) for his new record, which reminds me that I went to the video release celebration last night and (re) discovered[I played bass](http://mikeacerbo.bandcamp.com/) on that track! Blast from the past … it’s probably been a decade since we recorded some of those initial parts but the song is good as ever and Mike did a wonderful job not only writing it but arranging it, singing it, playing guitar on it, and generally making it happen.

As he should, since it is his thing. Go Mike!
