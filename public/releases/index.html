What the work produced

Releases

Records, films, apps and writing. The same work also appears as Conspiracies, which is the collaboration that made it, and as Appearances, which is where it was played.

Aural

  • I Just Believe in Christmas. 2015. A Christmas song sung by Michael Patrick Flanagan Smith, words finished with Jesse Schoen, Virginia Piazza and Doron Lev.

    Bandcamp · Lyric video

  • Reflections, Vol. 1. 2015. His first release of improvised duets, subtitled A Noise Akin to a Flight of Deranged Swallows in Combat.

    Apple Music

  • Lulu. 2011. The Lou Reed and Metallica album, grown out of the score written for Robert Wilson’s Berliner Ensemble production.

    Junior Dad

  • The Creation of the Universe. 2009. Lou Reed’s Metal Machine Trio, live. The two REDCAT nights nearly whole.

    Apple Music · Discogs

  • The Family Album. 2008. Lucibel Crater, the trio of Leah Coloff, Paul Chuffo and Sarth Calhoun.

  • Suspension. 2001. Number19, the band he formed in 1999 with Tony Diodore, Leah Coloff and Marcus Righter.

    Spotify

Optical

  • The 2026 animated shorts. 2026. Seven films made at Third Wall Studio with Doron Lev, Jacob McCoy, Savas and Jonathan Arons, built in Burlap.

    Third Wall · YouTube

  • Burlap. 2026. A native macOS infinite canvas for visual reference prompting.

    burlap.app · Introducing Burlap

  • Lou Reed Live at Lollapalooza 2009. 2011. An official DVD of the 2009 festival set. The credits read “Sarth Calhoun – electronics & continuum.”

    Credits

    bookofsarth.com · The Verge
