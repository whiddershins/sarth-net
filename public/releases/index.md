What the work produced

# Releases

Records, films, apps and writing. The same work also appears as [Conspiracies](/conspiracies/), which is the collaboration that made it, and as [Appearances](/appearances/), which is where it was played.

## Aural

- **[I Just Believe in Christmas](/conspiracies/i-just-believe-in-christmas/)**. 2015. A Christmas song sung by Michael Patrick Flanagan Smith, words finished with Jesse Schoen, Virginia Piazza and Doron Lev.

[Bandcamp](https://sarth.bandcamp.com/track/i-just-believe-in-christmas) · [Lyric video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXqYHjmmev0)

- **[Reflections, Vol. 1](/conspiracies/reflections-vol-1/)**. 2015. His first release of improvised duets, subtitled A Noise Akin to a Flight of Deranged Swallows in Combat.

[Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/album/reflections-vol-1-a-noise-akin-to-a-flight/1061852160)

- **[Lulu](/conspiracies/lulu/)**. 2011. The Lou Reed and Metallica album, grown out of the score written for Robert Wilson’s Berliner Ensemble production.

[Junior Dad](/conspiracies/junior-dad/)

- **[The Creation of the Universe](/conspiracies/metal-machine-trio/)**. 2009. Lou Reed’s Metal Machine Trio, live. The two REDCAT nights nearly whole.

[Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/album/the-creation-of-the-universe/315196713) · [Discogs](https://www.discogs.com/release/1583151-Lou-Reed-s-Metal-Machine-Trio-The-Creation-Of-The-Universe)

- **[The Family Album](/conspiracies/lucibel-crater/)**. 2008. Lucibel Crater, the trio of Leah Coloff, Paul Chuffo and Sarth Calhoun.

- **Suspension**. 2001. Number19, the band he formed in 1999 with Tony Diodore, Leah Coloff and Marcus Righter.

[Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/album/0EBi9RJO4j2cGeL0CPtYW5)

## Optical

- **[The 2026 animated shorts](/work/)**. 2026. Seven films made at Third Wall Studio with Doron Lev, Jacob McCoy, Savas and Jonathan Arons, built in Burlap.

[Third Wall](https://thirdwallstudio.com) · [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@burlap_app)

- **Burlap**. 2026. A native macOS infinite canvas for visual reference prompting.

[burlap.app](https://burlap.app) · [Introducing Burlap](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CP8939UdzSo)

- **[Lou Reed Live at Lollapalooza 2009](/appearances/)**. 2011. An official DVD of the 2009 festival set. The credits read “Sarth Calhoun – electronics & continuum.”

[Credits](https://loureed.com/news/lou-reed-live-at-lollapalooza-2009-dvd/)

- **[The Book of Sarth](/conspiracies/book-of-sarth/)**. 2012. A graphic novel and concept album built as one iPad app. His first full-length solo release.

[bookofsarth.com](https://bookofsarth.com) · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/2013/1/3/3828314/the-book-of-sarth-ipad-app-graphic-novel-concept-album)

## Lexical

- **[Transmissions](/transmissions/)**. Essays on data engineering, SQL, visual reference prompting and the tools behind the work.
