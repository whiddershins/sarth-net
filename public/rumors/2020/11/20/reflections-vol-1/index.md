# Reflections, Vol. 1

**This is the old page, kept as it was.** Today’s page: [Reflections, Vol. 1](/conspiracies/reflections-vol-1/).

Reflections, Vol1 (A Noise Akin to a Flight of Deranged Swallows in Combat) is my first release of improvised duets (and the 4th piece is a trio, which breaks the rule).

The name came from my friend Scott Hamptopn (a.ka. Exaltron) who took it from a review of one of my Metal Machine Trio performances. I love it, and I loved the idea of having a band with an absurdly long name.

Exaltron is featured on the last track, playing trumper, guitar, guitar-bass, and live looping madness.

The third track, Softly Questioning, because the main melody used in the film ‘Poughkeepsie’ written and directed by Joe Kelly.
