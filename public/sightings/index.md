# Sightings

A record of shows and installations. *The Creation of the Universe* comes from two nights at REDCAT, October 2–3, 2008. The later ambisonic installation uses a different recording, made at the Gramercy show on April 24, 2009.

## Spine

1. **Metal Machine Trio (Unclassified) at REDCAT, Los Angeles · Oct 2–3, 2008.** World premiere billed as *Unclassified: [Lou Reed](/conspirators/lou-reed/) and [Ulrich Krieger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulrich_Krieger)*. Three performances: Thu 2 Oct 8:30pm, added late show Thu 2 Oct 10:30pm, Fri 3 Oct 8:30pm. Me on Continuum and live processing. Official live album *The Creation of the Universe* is the unedited recording of those two nights.
2. **Metal Machine Trio, Blender Theater at Gramercy, New York · Apr 23–24, 2009.** First NYC stand. [John Zorn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Zorn) sat in on the 24th. This night’s recording later became the CSULB / Cranbrook ambisonic installation.
3. **Yellow Pony with Lou Reed and [Laurie Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurie_Anderson) · Europe, Jul and Aug–Sep 2009.** Eight-city tour billed *The Yellow Pony and Other Songs and Stories*. Me on Continuum and live voice re-sampling, center stage. Two short tours.
4. **Lou Reed at Lollapalooza, Grant Park, Chicago · Aug 9, 2009.** Day three. Metal Machine Trio played a ten minute noise interlude inside Reed’s set. Me on Continuum and live processing.
5. **Metal Machine Trio Europe · “A Night of Deep Noise,” Apr 17–30, 2010.** Nine dates, UK through Palma de Mallorca.
6. **Metal Machine Trio, [Sydney Opera House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sydney_Opera_House) (Opera Theatre) · May 30, 2010.** [Vivid LIVE](/conspiracies/vivid-live/). Anderson’s *Transitory Life* was a separate Drama Theatre program, June 1 and 2.
7. **Metal Machine Trio, Sesc Pinheiros, São Paulo · Nov 20–21, 2010.** Two nights.
8. **Ambisonic installation *The Creation of the Universe*** · CSULB University Art Museum, Long Beach, 27 Jan–Apr 15, 2012; restaged [Cranbrook Art Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cranbrook_Art_Museum), Bloomfield Hills, Nov 21, 2015–Mar 26, 2016. Playback of the Gramercy 2009 recording.

The two nights in São Paulo were the last Metal Machine Trio concerts.

## Lulu

Electronics on *[Lulu](/conspiracies/lulu/)*, 2011, and Continuum drones on the tapes made before [Metallica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metallica), as David Fricke described them. I directed rehearsals of the score for Robert Wilson’s Berlin *Lulu*. [Junior Dad](/conspiracies/junior-dad/) began at Lou’s apartment with Rob Wasserman and Kyma.

- **November 2011** · The first live performances of the *Lulu* material, with Lou Reed and Metallica: *Later... with Jools Holland* in London, *Taratata* in Paris, and five songs in Cologne. Got to play some of these Lulu tracks on the Jools Holland show … the first live performances of this material! and Had a great time performing 5 songs from Lulu in Cologne … it was such an intense experience. From my posts of November 9 and 12, 2011.
