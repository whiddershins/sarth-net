# Reaktor Live, June 27, 2023

Maximizing Growth Through Accessible and Actionable Data: a webinar with Michele Stone of Paramount, moderated by Ian Fosbery.

Reaktor Live is [Reaktor](https://reaktor.com)’s webinar series. The session on June 27, 2023 was “Maximizing Growth Through Accessible and Actionable Data”: Michele Stone, VP of Advanced Advertising Product and Planning at Paramount, and me, Reaktor’s engineering lead and lead data architect on that account, with Ian Fosbery of Reaktor moderating. Reaktor put the recording on its YouTube channel on July 14, 2023.

I was a data engineer at Reaktor, embedded in Paramount’s Advanced Advertising data organization; the team built and ran the advertising data pipeline. That work is on [Work](/work/), and the pattern behind it is written up in the [external-tables series](/transmissions/external-tables/).
