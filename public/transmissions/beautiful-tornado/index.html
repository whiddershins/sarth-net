Beautiful Tornado

Beautiful Tornado is the podcast Dom Bouffard and I made: six long conversations with musicians and artists, recorded in 2020 and 2021. It is on YouTube and Apple Podcasts.

It’s like a dirty secret. A place where musicians and artists talk about the kind of stuff they love to talk about, but their girlfriends / boyfriends don’t. We’ll be chatting to amazing and original creators around the world about new releases, how they think about what they do, whether robots will kill us all and if peyote is as good on cornflakes as on Rice Crispies... that kinda thing..
