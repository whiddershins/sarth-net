# Rich Nav's Positivity Mission

Episode 4 of the Beautiful Tornado podcast features Rich Nav and his Positivity Mission.

Songwriter, poet, firefighter, transatlantic sailor, dad… Richard Naviasky @the1nav does a LOT of stuff and then sings enchanting songs about it… Sarth and Dom get him to take time out from his #positivity mission and talk about round bottoms, why music’s better than art, and crapping off the side of boats…
