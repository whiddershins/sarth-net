Aural

Records and songs.

  • Singles

    2020 to 2025. Eleven singles as Sarth on Noise | Groove, from “Squishy | Bass” to “Softly Questioning (Reprise),” and one as Catfish Calhoun and Friends.

    Spotify · Apple Music

  • Beautiful Tornado

    2020 to 2021. The podcast with Dom Bouffard: six conversations with musicians and artists.

    YouTube · Apple Podcasts

  • I Just Believe in Christmas

    2015. A Christmas song sung by Michael Patrick Flanagan Smith, words finished with Jesse Schoen, Virginia Piazza and Doron Lev.

    Bandcamp

  • Reflections, Vol. 1

    2015. My first release of improvised duets, subtitled A Noise Akin to a Flight of Deranged Swallows in Combat.

    Apple Music

  • Transmissions from the Book of Sarth

    2013. Four pieces from the music of The Book of Sarth, released as an EP a year after the app.

    Spotify

  • Solsbury Hill

    2013. Lou Reed’s recording of the Peter Gabriel song for And I’ll Scratch Yours. I produced it and played electronics.

  • Awakening (To Blacklist)

    2012. A track from The Book of Sarth. Its melody is made out of a recording of 60 cycle hum.

    bookofsarth.com

  • Lulu

    2011. The Lou Reed and Metallica album, grown out of the score Lou Reed and I wrote for Robert Wilson’s Berliner Ensemble production.

  • Junior Dad

    2011. The closing track of Lulu, nineteen and a half minutes. It began at Lou Reed’s apartment with Rob Wasserman’s bowed electric upright running through Kyma.

    Apple Music

  • Peggy Sue

    2011. Lou Reed’s recording of the Buddy Holly song for Rave On Buddy Holly. I played bass on it, a Moog Little Phatty.

  • Power and Serenity

    2010. Six pieces of tai chi meditation music composed and performed with Lou Reed for Master Ren Guangyi’s DVD, and only there: no CD was ever released. The first thing we worked on together.

    loureed.com, archived

  • The Creation of the Universe

    2009. Lou Reed’s Metal Machine Trio, live. The two REDCAT nights nearly whole.

    Apple Music · Discogs

  • The Family Album

    2008. Lucibel Crater, the trio of Leah Coloff, Paul Chuffo and me.

  • Miracles

    2007. Lucibel Crater’s EP, the year before The Family Album.

  • Suspension

    2001. Number19, the band I formed in 1999 with Tony Diodore, Leah Coloff and Mark Righter.

    Spotify
