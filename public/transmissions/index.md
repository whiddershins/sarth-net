# Transmissions

What went out: records, films, apps and writing. The collaborations that made them are [Conspiracies](/conspiracies/), and where they were played is [Sightings](/sightings/).

## Aural

Records and songs.

- ## [Singles](/transmissions/singles/)

  2020 to 2025. Eleven singles as Sarth on Noise | Groove, from “Squishy | Bass” to “Softly Questioning (Reprise),” and one as Catfish Calhoun and Friends.

  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/6uUtOazhiVXfNU8KAglFv9) · [Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/artist/sarth/1061852162)

- ## [Beautiful Tornado](/transmissions/beautiful-tornado/)

  2020 to 2021. The podcast with Dom Bouffard: six conversations with musicians and artists.

  [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLmXj9m6I5JooEjI2skmu1LdMlhlmYaTfb) · [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/beautiful-tornado/id1553841403)

- ## [I Just Believe in Christmas](/conspiracies/i-just-believe-in-christmas/)

  2015. A Christmas song sung by Michael Patrick Flanagan Smith, words finished with Jesse Schoen, Virginia Piazza and Doron Lev.

  [Bandcamp](https://sarth.bandcamp.com/track/i-just-believe-in-christmas)

- ## [Reflections, Vol. 1](/conspiracies/reflections-vol-1/)

  2015. My first release of improvised duets, subtitled *A Noise Akin to a Flight of Deranged Swallows in Combat*.

  [Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/album/reflections-vol-1-a-noise-akin-to-a-flight/1061852160)

- ## [Transmissions from the Book of Sarth](/transmissions/transmissions-from-the-book-of-sarth/)

  2013. Four pieces from the music of *The Book of Sarth*, released as an EP a year after the app.

  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/album/2AK3ADNcGHccdecSkjV43k)

- ## [Solsbury Hill](/transmissions/solsbury-hill/)

  2013. Lou Reed’s recording of the Peter Gabriel song for *And I’ll Scratch Yours*. I produced it and played electronics.

- ## [Awakening (To Blacklist)](/conspiracies/awakening-to-blacklist/)

  2012. A track from *The Book of Sarth*. Its melody is made out of a recording of 60 cycle hum.

  [bookofsarth.com](https://bookofsarth.com)

- ## [Lulu](/conspiracies/lulu/)

  2011. The Lou Reed and Metallica album, grown out of the score Lou Reed and I wrote for Robert Wilson’s Berliner Ensemble production.

- ## [Junior Dad](/conspiracies/junior-dad/)

  2011. The closing track of *Lulu*, nineteen and a half minutes. It began at Lou Reed’s apartment with Rob Wasserman’s bowed electric upright running through Kyma.

  [Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/album/junior-dad/467890760?i=467890784)

- ## [Peggy Sue](/transmissions/peggy-sue/)

  2011. Lou Reed’s recording of the Buddy Holly song for *Rave On Buddy Holly*. I played bass on it, a Moog Little Phatty.

- ## [Power and Serenity](/conspiracies/power-and-serenity/)

  2010. Six pieces of tai chi meditation music composed and performed with Lou Reed for Master Ren Guangyi’s DVD, and only there: no CD was ever released. The first thing we worked on together.

  [loureed.com, archived](https://web.archive.org/web/20101230193716/http://loureed.com/power/)

- ## [The Creation of the Universe](/transmissions/the-creation-of-the-universe/)

  2009. Lou Reed’s Metal Machine Trio, live. The two REDCAT nights nearly whole.

  [Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/album/the-creation-of-the-universe/315196713) · [Discogs](https://www.discogs.com/release/1583151-Lou-Reed-s-Metal-Machine-Trio-The-Creation-Of-The-Universe)

- ## [The Family Album](/transmissions/the-family-album/)

  2008. Lucibel Crater, the trio of Leah Coloff, Paul Chuffo and me.

- ## [Miracles](/transmissions/miracles/)

  2007. Lucibel Crater’s EP, the year before *The Family Album*.

- ## [Suspension](/transmissions/suspension/)

  2001. Number19, the band I formed in 1999 with Tony Diodore, Leah Coloff and Mark Righter.

  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/album/0EBi9RJO4j2cGeL0CPtYW5)

## Lexical

Essays and writing.

- ## [DuckDB, where have you been all my life](/transmissions/duckdb-where-have-you-been-all-my-life/)

  Sep 22, 2026. Asking Claude how much an artist spent on a project in Burlap, answered straight from JSON files with DuckDB. Plus the whole append-only pipeline pattern, on a laptop.

- ## [External Tables, Partitions, CTEs, Window Functions, and Partitions](/transmissions/external-tables/)

  Tutorial series, five parts. Building an append-only data pipeline on external tables, with window functions for the current truth and runtime type checking at the boundary.

- ## [Window functions, the mental model](/transmissions/window-functions/)

  SQL. Compare them to aggregates, PARTITION BY vs GROUP BY, ROW_NUMBER vs RANK, and why the window grows.

- ## [SQL as the data language](/transmissions/sql-as-the-data-language/)

  SQL. Views, CTEs, joins, and why everything is a table (which is also not true).

- ## [You might not need Pandas](/transmissions/you-might-not-need-pandas/)

  Essay. Most day to day Pandas work is better done in SQL, with a side by side example, and where Pandas still earns its place.

- ## [Why Python is the default for data work](/transmissions/why-python-is-the-default-for-data-work/)

  Essay. It is what people know, plus an ecosystem written in C, C++ and CUDA. What that does and does not tell you about building pipelines.

- ## [Modern Postgres is shockingly capable](/transmissions/modern-postgres/)

  Essay. JSONB, horizontal scaling, and the rise and fall of MongoDB.

- ## [Node vs Rails: agnostic vs batteries included](/transmissions/node-vs-rails/)

  Essay. The case that an agnostic server beats batteries included, and why I am not sure I believe it.

- ## [Visual Reference Prompting](/transmissions/visual-reference-prompting/)

  Jul 31, 2026. How original images can hold a subject together, bend a style, and open up stranger places to go next.

- ## [About Awakening (To Blacklist)](/transmissions/about-awakening-to-blacklist/)

  Nov 21, 2020. How the track’s melody came out of a recording of 60 cycle hum: a frequency shifter on the Spacebirds preset, wet/dry automation, and a happy accident with two frozen drum tracks. With the audio examples.
