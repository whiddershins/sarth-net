Lou Reed Live at Lollapalooza 2009

Lou Reed Live at Lollapalooza 2009 is the official DVD of Lou Reed’s set at Lollapalooza in Grant Park, Chicago, on Sunday, August 9, 2009, released July 19, 2011. The band was Mike Rathke and Steve Hunter on guitars, Kevin Hearn on keyboards, Rob Wasserman on bass, Tony “Thunder” Smith on drums, Ulrich Krieger on saxophone, and me on electronics and Continuum.

Reed, on the other hand, brought the weekend’s most audacious music, a set laced with bile, violence and feedback, never more so than when he threaded a raging “Paranoia Key of E” into a pile-driving “Waiting for the Man.”

Greg Kot, Chicago Tribune, August 10, 2009

The set

Day three, to a capacity crowd of forty thousand. Rolling Stone noted Reed ran fifteen minutes over. Inside the set, Metal Machine Trio played a ten minute noise interlude between the songs. The date is on Sightings.

The interlude

Ulrich Krieger, writing after Reed died: At 2009’s Lollapalooza MM3 played a ten minute noise interlude between well known Reed songs to the frantic cheers of a large audience. And of the tours that followed: On subsequent tours with his newly revised rock group, including Sarth and me, he would play MM3 recordings before and after shows.

CreditsFrom loureed.com: Mike Rathke, guitar. Steve Hunter, guitar. Kevin Hearn, keyboards. Rob Wasserman, bass. Tony “Thunder” Smith, drums. Ulrich Krieger, saxophone. Sarth Calhoun, electronics and continuum. Discogs lists it on Sister Ray Enterprises, Sarth credited for Continuum fingerboard and processing.

Rob Wasserman’s upright electric bass, Kevin Hearn’s string/horn patches, Sarth Calhoun’s invisible Macbookery, and guitarist Steve Hunter’s fiery if decidedly inside-the-box solos.

Jesse Jarnow on the same band a year earlier, The Village Voice, May 6, 2008

Conspiracies

This is Lou Reed’s song band on film, with Metal Machine Trio inside it. Rob Wasserman and Ulrich Krieger have their own pages. My rig that day was the Continuum and Kyma.
