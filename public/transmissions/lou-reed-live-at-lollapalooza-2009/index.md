# *Lou Reed Live at Lollapalooza 2009*

*Lou Reed Live at Lollapalooza 2009* is the official DVD of [Lou Reed](/conspirators/lou-reed/)’s set at Lollapalooza in Grant Park, Chicago, on Sunday, August 9, 2009, released July 19, 2011. The band was Mike Rathke and Steve Hunter on guitars, Kevin Hearn on keyboards, [Rob Wasserman](/conspirators/rob-wasserman/) on bass, Tony “Thunder” Smith on drums, [Ulrich Krieger](/conspirators/ulrich-krieger/) on saxophone, and me on electronics and Continuum.

![Sarth Calhoun performing at Lollapalooza, a bass headstock in the foreground.](/images/lolla-dvd-show.jpg)

Sarth Calhoun at Lollapalooza, August 9, 2009.

> Reed, on the other hand, brought the weekend’s most audacious music, a set laced with bile, violence and feedback, never more so than when he threaded a raging “Paranoia Key of E” into a pile-driving “Waiting for the Man.”
> 
> Greg Kot, [Chicago Tribune, August 10, 2009](https://www.chicagotribune.com/2009/08/10/lollapalooza-does-it-again/)

## The set

Day three, to a capacity crowd of forty thousand. [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/lou-reed-snarls-vampire-weekend-bounce-on-eclectic-lollapalooza-day-three-2-65494/) noted Reed ran fifteen minutes over. Inside the set, [Metal Machine Trio](/conspiracies/metal-machine-trio/) played a ten minute noise interlude between the songs. The date is on [Sightings](/sightings/).

## The interlude

Ulrich Krieger, writing after Reed died: At 2009’s Lollapalooza MM3 played a ten minute noise interlude between well known Reed songs to the frantic cheers of a large audience. And of the tours that followed: On subsequent tours with his newly revised rock group, including Sarth and me, he would play MM3 recordings before and after shows.
