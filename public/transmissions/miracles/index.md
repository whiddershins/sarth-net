# *Miracles*

*Miracles* is an EP by [Lucibel Crater](/conspiracies/lucibel-crater/), the Brooklyn trio of [Leah Coloff](/conspirators/leah-coloff/), [Paul Chuffo](/conspirators/paul-chuffo/) and me, released in 2007, the year before [*The Family Album*](/transmissions/the-family-album/).

## Conspiracies

Made by [Lucibel Crater](/conspiracies/lucibel-crater/). The band’s full-length is [*The Family Album*](/transmissions/the-family-album/).
