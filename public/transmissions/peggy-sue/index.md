# “Peggy Sue”

“Peggy Sue” is [Lou Reed](/conspirators/lou-reed/)’s recording of the Buddy Holly song for *Rave On Buddy Holly*, the tribute compilation released by Fantasy and Concord in June 2011, alongside Paul McCartney, Patti Smith, Florence and the Machine and the Black Keys. I play bass on it, a Moog Little Phatty. Three minutes nineteen.

## What was written about it

Apple Music’s note on the album calls Reed’s take the sound of a cement mixer grinding the tune to a nub. Far Out remembers it as raw, punky. It sits at track seventeen of nineteen.

> Lou Reed tackles Buddy Holly’s classic hit ‘Peggy Sue’ the best way he knows how -- with distortion, fuzz and feedback.
> 
> [Ultimate Classic Rock](https://ultimateclassicrock.com/lou-reed-peggy-sue-song-review/), song review, 2011

The compilation: Nineteen acts on Buddy Holly songs, 2011. Fantasy Records with MPL. Reed’s is the only track Sarth plays on.

## Conspiracies

Recorded while I was in [Lou Reed’s band](/conspiracies/lou-reed-song-band/), which I joined in 2008. The bass here is a Moog synthesizer, not the electric upright [Rob Wasserman](/conspirators/rob-wasserman/) played on stage.
