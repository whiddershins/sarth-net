Singles

Eleven singles I released as Sarth between January 2020 and May 2025 on my own Noise | Groove label, and one as Catfish Calhoun and Friends. All are on Spotify and Apple Music. Newest first.

Softly Questioning (Reprise)

May 20, 2025. 4:19. A return to “Softly Questioning” from Reflections, Vol. 1, the piece whose melody became the main theme of Joe Kelly’s film Poughkeepsie. On Spotify and Apple Music.

Ego Streaked

November 28, 2022. 0:44. Forty-four seconds. On Spotify and Apple Music.

Shimmering Angels (in 40 years)

September 2, 2022. 4:02. Spoken word. On Spotify and Apple Music.

Failure is OK

May 16, 2022. 4:48. On Spotify and Apple Music.

Sometimes You Need Sun

May 3, 2022. 4:06. Released as Catfish Calhoun and Friends, with Doron Lev. On Spotify and Apple Music.

My Empty Father

January 6, 2022. 3:18. On Spotify and Apple Music.

For Lovers

October 30, 2020. 8:32. With Jacob McCoy. The 2015 Linnstrument and Continuum duet from Introspections, released as a single five years later. On Spotify and Apple Music.

On March 2nd, 2015, Jacob and I got together to record some Linnstrument / Continuum Fingerboard duets. It was also Lou Reed’s birthday, and this piece reminded me of things we explored together. Drones, close intervals, sum and difference frequencies. And Lou loved his Continuum Fingerboard. So this is dedicated to him.

Everyth Everything (Sarth Mix)

October 23, 2020. 4:25. My own mix of “Everything Everything.” On Spotify and Apple Music.

Everything Everything

April 9, 2020. 4:33. On Spotify and Apple Music.

I feel this might be the best thing I’ve ever made. I worked for a long time on this song, but I could never finish it. And then one day it was done.
