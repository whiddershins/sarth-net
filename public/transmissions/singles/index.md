## For Lovers

October 30, 2020. 8:32. With Jacob McCoy. The 2015 Linnstrument and Continuum duet from [Introspections](/transmissions/introspections/), released as a single five years later. On [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/album/1eYowG2sDJhykbBMc1nk3g) and [Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/album/1537204819).

On March 2nd, 2015, Jacob and I got together to record some Linnstrument / Continuum Fingerboard duets. It was also Lou Reed’s birthday, and this piece reminded me of things we explored together. Drones, close intervals, sum and difference frequencies. And Lou loved his Continuum Fingerboard. So this is dedicated to him.

[Embedded player](https://open.spotify.com/embed/album/1eYowG2sDJhykbBMc1nk3g)
