# *The Family Album*

*The Family Album* is the only full-length by [Lucibel Crater](/conspiracies/lucibel-crater/), the Brooklyn trio of [Leah Coloff](/conspirators/leah-coloff/), [Paul Chuffo](/conspirators/paul-chuffo/) and me. Ten songs, released February 12, 2008 on Searching Eye Records and mixed by [Bryce Goggin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryce_Goggin).

[Lou Reed](/conspirators/lou-reed/) plays guitar on “Threadbare Funeral.” It is the only guitar on the record.

![Cover of The Family Album by Lucibel Crater.](/images/art-the-family-album.jpg)

Cover, *The Family Album*, 2008.

The songs: 1. There Was a Time. 2. Your Mean Mouth. 3. Into the Bushes. 4. Threadbare Funeral, featuring Lou Reed. 5. Next of Kin. 6. Blue Stationwagon. 7. Where You Are. 8. Bones. 9. Swimmers. 10. Could_i.

## The songs

Ross Christopher at awaken music picked out “Where You Are,” “There was a Time” and “Threadbare Funeral.” MP3 Troll noted the Lou Reed guitar in May 2008, before the reviews. Coloff sings and plays cello; Chuffo plays drums; I play keys, bass, loops, [Kyma](/devices/kyma/) and the [Continuum](/devices/continuum/).

Vogue Italia, January 2009: Ten muscular tracks and only one guitar, Lou Reed’s.

## What was written about it

[Buzzbin Magazine](https://web.archive.org/web/20110621012417/http://www.buzzbinmagazine.com/home/2008/07/02/lucibel-crater-the-family-album/) reviewed it in July 2008: this is totally music to get high to. Simone Tempia wrote it up twice in Italy, in *Urban* in February 2009, where the Björk and White Stripes comparison comes from (Ci si può sentire qualcosa di Björk e magari dei White Stripes. Ma la verità è che il loro sound è unico e difficilmente incasellabile.), and in *Vogue Italia* that January. [Lost in Sound](http://lostinsound.org/lucibel-crater-not-your-dads-jazz/) interviewed the band in February 2010. The rest is on [Rumors](/rumors/).

## Conspiracies

Made by [Lucibel Crater](/conspiracies/lucibel-crater/), with [Leah Coloff](/conspirators/leah-coloff/), [Paul Chuffo](/conspirators/paul-chuffo/) and [Lou Reed](/conspirators/lou-reed/). The band’s earlier EP is [*Miracles*](/transmissions/miracles/), 2007. My tools on it are on [Devices](/devices/).
