# *Transmissions from the Book of Sarth*

*Transmissions from the Book of Sarth* is a four-track EP I released in 2013 from the music of [*The Book of Sarth*](/conspiracies/book-of-sarth/), the graphic novel and concept album I built as an iPad app the year before. The score was made with [Kyma](/devices/kyma/).

## Listen

Four pieces: “Awakening (To Blacklist),” “Table (If I Can),” “Primary Defenestration (Deforestation)” and “For Valentina (To Your Rescue).” The first has [its own page](/conspiracies/awakening-to-blacklist/): its melody is made out of a recording of 60 cycle hum. On [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/album/2AK3ADNcGHccdecSkjV43k).

## The hum

One of my favorite things about this track is the morph from the hum to a rhythmic/pitched pattern. There’s a scene in ‘Book of Sarth’ where the office workers are coming out of a daze, they are transitioning back in to reality, and the hum transforming really ended up matching this perfectly. (I don’t know which idea came first.)

I start with a recording of typical 60 cycle hum. This was pretty straightforward, I probably just messed with a 1/4” cable partially plugged into my Marshall amp, or something similar. I wanted to see if I could transform that sound into something.

The whole of that, with the audio examples, is on its own page: [About Awakening (To Blacklist)](/transmissions/about-awakening-to-blacklist/).
